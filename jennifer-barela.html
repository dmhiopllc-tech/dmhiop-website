Meet Jennifer

Compassionate Medical Care for Lasting Recovery

Jennifer Barela is a Psychiatric Certified Nurse Practitioner dedicated to helping individuals achieve lasting recovery through compassionate, evidence-based medical care.

Jennifer understands that substance use disorders and mental health conditions are medical conditions that deserve the same professional treatment, respect, and individualized care as any other health concern. She believes every client deserves to be heard, understood, and actively involved in developing a treatment plan that reflects their unique goals and circumstances.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Jennifer provides psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) while working closely with the counseling team to ensure every client receives comprehensive, whole-person care.

Her approach combines clinical expertise with empathy, helping clients feel comfortable asking questions, understanding their treatment options, and taking an active role in their recovery journey.

Professional Background

Dedicated to Mental Health & Addiction Medicine

Jennifer has devoted her career to improving the lives of individuals living with mental health conditions and substance use disorders.

Throughout her career, she has cared for individuals experiencing depression, anxiety, trauma, bipolar disorder, opioid use disorder, alcohol use disorder, stimulant use disorder, and co-occurring behavioral health conditions.

Her experience allows her to evaluate each client's physical health, emotional well-being, psychiatric needs, and recovery goals to develop individualized treatment plans that support long-term success.

Jennifer believes medication should never be viewed as the entire solution—but rather one valuable tool within a comprehensive recovery program.

Medication-Assisted Treatment

MAT: A Critical Component of Recovery

Jennifer is a passionate advocate for Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT), an evidence-based approach that combines FDA-approved medications with counseling and behavioral therapies to treat substance use disorders.

MAT Services at Desert Mountain Healing

  • Comprehensive psychiatric and medical evaluations
  • MAT for opioid use disorder (Suboxone/buprenorphine)
  • MAT for alcohol use disorder (naltrexone, Vivitrol)
  • Medication management for co-occurring mental health conditions
  • Ongoing monitoring, adjustment, and support
  • Collaboration with counselors, therapists, and case managers

Jennifer works closely with each client to determine whether MAT is appropriate for their specific situation, ensuring that medication is integrated seamlessly into a broader recovery plan that includes therapy, peer support, and lifestyle changes.

Psychiatric Services

Comprehensive Mental Health Care

In addition to MAT, Jennifer provides comprehensive psychiatric care for clients experiencing co-occurring mental health conditions.

Conditions Treated

  • Depression and mood disorders
  • Anxiety and panic disorders
  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
  • Co-occurring substance use and mental health disorders

Jennifer understands that many individuals struggling with addiction also face mental health challenges, and she is skilled at addressing both issues simultaneously through integrated, evidence-based treatment.

Treatment Philosophy

Client-Centered, Compassionate Care

Jennifer's treatment philosophy is rooted in respect, collaboration, and individualized care. She believes that clients should be active participants in their own treatment—not passive recipients of medical directives.

Core Values

  • Respect: Every client deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion, and professionalism
  • Collaboration: Treatment decisions should be made together, with clients fully informed and empowered
  • Whole-Person Care: Addressing physical health, mental health, and social circumstances as interconnected
  • Evidence-Based Practice: Using proven medical approaches backed by research and clinical experience
  • Non-Judgment: Creating a safe, supportive environment free from stigma and shame

Jennifer takes the time to listen to each client's concerns, answer questions thoroughly, and ensure they understand their treatment options and feel comfortable with the plan moving forward.

Jennifer was drawn to Desert Mountain Healing IOP because of its commitment to providing truly integrated, client-centered treatment. She's proud to work alongside a team of dedicated professionals who share her values of compassion, respect, and evidence-based practice, and she's excited to be part of a program that is making a real difference in the lives of individuals and families in Rio Rancho and beyond.
