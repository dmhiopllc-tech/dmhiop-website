Meet Jennifer

Compassionate Medical Care for Lasting Recovery

Jennifer Barela is a Psychiatric Certified Nurse Practitioner dedicated to helping individuals achieve lasting recovery through compassionate, evidence-based medical care.

Jennifer understands that substance use disorders and mental health conditions are medical conditions that deserve the same professional treatment, respect, and individualized care as any other health concern. She believes every client deserves to be heard, understood, and actively involved in developing a treatment plan that reflects their unique goals and circumstances.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Jennifer provides psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) while working closely with the counseling team to ensure every client receives comprehensive, whole-person care.

Her approach combines clinical expertise with empathy, helping clients feel comfortable asking questions, understanding their treatment options, and taking an active role in their recovery journey.

Professional Background

Dedicated to Mental Health & Addiction Medicine

Jennifer has devoted her career to improving the lives of individuals living with mental health conditions and substance use disorders.

Throughout her career, she has cared for individuals experiencing depression, anxiety, trauma, bipolar disorder, opioid use disorder, alcohol use disorder, stimulant use disorder, and co-occurring behavioral health conditions.

Her experience allows her to evaluate each client's physical health, emotional well-being, psychiatric needs, and recovery goals to develop individualized treatment plans that support long-term success.

Jennifer believes medication should never be viewed as the entire solution—but rather one valuable tool within a comprehensive recovery program.

Jennifer's Treatment Philosophy

Treating the Whole Person

Jennifer believes successful recovery begins with treating the whole person.

Medication can reduce cravings, stabilize mood, and improve overall functioning, but lasting recovery also requires counseling, education, healthy relationships, accountability, and continued personal growth.

Her philosophy is built on four principles:

1. Compassion First

Every client deserves to be treated with dignity, empathy, and respect.

2. Individualized Care

No two recovery journeys are the same. Every treatment plan should reflect the client's unique history, strengths, medical needs, and personal goals.

3. Education Creates Empowerment

Clients should fully understand their diagnosis, medications, treatment options, and recovery plan so they can make informed decisions about their care.

4. Recovery Is Possible

Jennifer believes every person has the capacity to recover with the right medical support, therapeutic services, and commitment to personal growth.

Jennifer's Role on Your Recovery Team

Integrated Medical Care

Jennifer works closely with counselors, therapists, peer support specialists, case managers, and clinical leadership to ensure every client's medical care is fully integrated into their overall treatment plan.

Her responsibilities include:

  • Psychiatric evaluations
  • MAT assessments
  • Medication management
  • Monitoring treatment progress
  • Adjusting medications when clinically appropriate
  • Coordinating care with the treatment team
  • Supporting long-term recovery planning

Jennifer believes the best outcomes occur when medical providers and behavioral health professionals work together toward the same goal—helping clients build healthy, fulfilling lives in recovery.

What to Expect During Your First MAT Appointment

A Welcoming, Comprehensive Evaluation

Beginning Medication-Assisted Treatment can feel overwhelming. Jennifer strives to create a welcoming environment where clients feel comfortable discussing their concerns and asking questions.

During your first appointment, you may expect:

  • A comprehensive review of your medical and substance use history
  • Discussion of your recovery goals and current challenges
  • Evaluation of withdrawal symptoms and cravings
  • Review of current medications and overall health
  • Assessment to determine whether MAT is appropriate
  • Development of an individualized treatment plan
  • Coordination with your counselor and treatment team
  • Time to ask questions and understand your treatment options

Every treatment plan is personalized. Medication recommendations are based on clinical evaluation, medical history, and each client's unique recovery goals.

MAT Is More Than Medication

Comprehensive Integrated Treatment

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Medication-Assisted Treatment is never viewed as a stand-alone service.

Jennifer believes medication is most effective when combined with comprehensive behavioral healthcare.

Clients receiving MAT may also participate in:

  • Intensive Outpatient Programming (IOP)
  • Individual counseling
  • Group therapy
  • Relapse prevention planning
  • Case management
  • Family support
  • Recovery education
  • Long-term recovery planning

This integrated approach addresses both the physical and emotional aspects of recovery, helping clients build a strong foundation for lasting success.
