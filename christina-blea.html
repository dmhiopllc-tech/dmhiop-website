Meet Christina

Compassionate Care for Lasting Recovery

Christina Blea is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome addiction, improve their mental health, and build meaningful, lasting recovery.

She believes every individual deserves to be treated with compassion, dignity, and respect, regardless of the challenges that have brought them to treatment. Christina understands that addiction often affects every aspect of a person's life, including relationships, emotional well-being, employment, physical health, and self-worth. Her goal is to help clients address these underlying issues while developing the skills necessary to create lasting change.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Christina provides individual counseling, group therapy, and collaborative treatment planning as part of a multidisciplinary team committed to whole-person care.

Her warm, client-centered approach encourages individuals to recognize their strengths, overcome barriers, and move confidently toward a healthier future.

Professional Background

Dedicated to Behavioral Health & Recovery

Christina has experience working with individuals facing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, family conflict, and other behavioral health concerns.

Throughout her career, she has helped clients navigate difficult life transitions, improve coping skills, strengthen relationships, and develop healthier ways of responding to life's challenges.

She believes successful recovery involves more than achieving sobriety—it requires healing emotionally, rebuilding confidence, reconnecting with family, and creating a life that feels meaningful and fulfilling.

Her collaborative approach allows clients to actively participate in developing treatment goals that reflect their personal values, strengths, and aspirations.

Therapeutic Approach

Evidence-Based, Client-Centered Care

Christina's therapeutic approach combines evidence-based interventions with a warm, empathetic style that helps clients feel heard, understood, and empowered.

Treatment Modalities

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Identifying and changing unhelpful thinking patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI): Supporting clients in exploring and resolving ambivalence about change
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Recognizing the impact of trauma and creating a safe therapeutic environment
  • Person-Centered Therapy: Honoring each client's unique experiences, values, and goals
  • Solution-Focused Brief Therapy: Emphasizing strengths and solutions rather than problems
  • Relapse Prevention: Building skills and strategies for maintaining long-term recovery

Christina believes that therapy is most effective when clients feel genuinely understood and respected, which is why she prioritizes building strong, trusting therapeutic relationships.

Areas of Focus

Comprehensive Behavioral Health Support

Christina provides counseling and support for a wide range of behavioral health challenges, including:

  • Substance use disorders (alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis)
  • Co-occurring mental health conditions (anxiety, depression, PTSD)
  • Trauma and adverse childhood experiences (ACEs)
  • Grief and loss
  • Relationship and family conflict
  • Life transitions and adjustment challenges
  • Self-esteem and identity issues
  • Relapse prevention and recovery maintenance

Her holistic approach recognizes that addiction rarely exists in isolation—it's often intertwined with mental health challenges, trauma, relationship difficulties, and other life stressors.

Individual & Group Therapy

Personalized Support, Community Connection

Christina provides both individual counseling and group therapy, recognizing that each format offers unique benefits for clients in recovery.

Individual Counseling

In one-on-one sessions, Christina works with clients to explore personal challenges, develop coping strategies, process difficult emotions, and create personalized recovery plans. Individual therapy offers a safe, confidential space to address issues that may be difficult to discuss in a group setting.

Group Therapy

Group therapy provides clients with the opportunity to connect with others who share similar experiences, reducing isolation and building a sense of community. Christina facilitates groups that focus on skill-building, relapse prevention, emotional regulation, and peer support.

Benefits of Group Therapy

  • Reduces feelings of isolation and shame
  • Provides peer support and accountability
  • Offers diverse perspectives and insights
  • Builds communication and interpersonal skills
  • Reinforces recovery concepts through shared learning
