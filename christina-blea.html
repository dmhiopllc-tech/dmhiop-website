Meet Christina

Compassionate Care for Lasting Recovery

Christina Blea is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome addiction, improve their mental health, and build meaningful, lasting recovery.

She believes every individual deserves to be treated with compassion, dignity, and respect, regardless of the challenges that have brought them to treatment. Christina understands that addiction often affects every aspect of a person's life, including relationships, emotional well-being, employment, physical health, and self-worth. Her goal is to help clients address these underlying issues while developing the skills necessary to create lasting change.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Christina provides individual counseling, group therapy, and collaborative treatment planning as part of a multidisciplinary team committed to whole-person care.

Her warm, client-centered approach encourages individuals to recognize their strengths, overcome barriers, and move confidently toward a healthier future.

Professional Background

Dedicated to Behavioral Health & Recovery

Christina has experience working with individuals facing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, family conflict, and other behavioral health concerns.

Throughout her career, she has helped clients navigate difficult life transitions, improve coping skills, strengthen relationships, and develop healthier ways of responding to life's challenges.

She believes successful recovery involves more than achieving sobriety—it requires healing emotionally, rebuilding confidence, reconnecting with family, and creating a life that feels meaningful and fulfilling.

Her collaborative approach allows clients to actively participate in developing treatment goals that reflect their personal values, strengths, and aspirations.

Education & Credentials

Professional Qualifications

  • Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW)
  • Ongoing professional development and continuing education
  • Evidence-based behavioral healthcare training

Christina remains committed to ongoing professional development and lifelong learning to provide clients with current, evidence-based behavioral healthcare.

Counseling Philosophy

Building on Strengths, Creating Change

Christina believes every person already possesses strengths that can serve as the foundation for recovery.

Her counseling philosophy is built upon four guiding principles:

1. Compassion Creates Safety

People grow when they feel accepted, respected, and supported without judgment.

2. Every Story Matters

Each person's life experiences are unique and deserve individualized treatment.

3. Recovery Is About More Than Sobriety

Long-term recovery includes emotional wellness, healthy relationships, purpose, and personal growth.

4. Lasting Change Happens Through Small Steps

Recovery is built one decision, one accomplishment, and one day at a time.

Christina's Role on Your Recovery Team

Primary Therapist & Treatment Partner

As a Primary Therapist, Christina works closely with clients to develop individualized treatment plans that address both addiction and the underlying emotional challenges contributing to substance use.

Her responsibilities include:

  • Individual therapy
  • Group facilitation
  • Treatment planning
  • Progress evaluations
  • Relapse prevention
  • Family support
  • Care coordination with the multidisciplinary treatment team
  • Ongoing recovery planning

Christina believes recovery is strongest when clients receive coordinated care that addresses every aspect of their well-being.

What Clients Can Expect

Working with Christina

Clients working with Christina can expect:

  • A safe, welcoming environment
  • Compassionate, respectful communication
  • Individualized treatment planning
  • Practical coping strategies
  • Honest encouragement
  • Evidence-based counseling
  • Support through every stage of recovery

She strives to help every client feel heard, valued, and capable of achieving lasting change.

Why Christina Chose Behavioral Health

A Calling to Serve & Heal

Christina entered the behavioral health profession because she believes helping people overcome adversity is one of the most meaningful ways to serve others.

She is inspired by the resilience she sees in her clients and considers it a privilege to support individuals as they rediscover hope, rebuild confidence, and create healthier futures for themselves and their families.

Therapeutic Approach

Insight & Practical Tools for Growth

Christina believes therapy should provide both insight and practical tools.

She works collaboratively with clients to:

  • Increase self-awareness
  • Build emotional resilience
  • Improve communication
  • Develop healthy coping skills
  • Strengthen relationships
  • Manage stress effectively
  • Prepare for lifelong recovery

Her goal is to empower every client to leave treatment with confidence, hope, and the skills needed to continue growing long after treatment ends.
