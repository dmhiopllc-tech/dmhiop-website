Sean Roberts

Co-Founder & Operations Director

"Recovery isn't just about overcoming addiction—it's about rediscovering purpose, rebuilding hope, and creating a life worth living."

Meet Sean

For Sean Roberts, recovery is more than a profession—it's a lifelong calling.

In 1987, Sean began his own recovery journey. Over the past 37+ years, he has dedicated his life to helping others find the same freedom, purpose, and hope that transformed his own life.

Sean didn't just want to create another addiction treatment center—he wanted to build a place where people are treated with the dignity, compassion, and respect they deserve. Together with his business partner Gary Gamboa, Sean co-founded Desert Mountain Healing IOP on principles they both knew were essential: dignity, compassion, respect, hope, and accountability.

As Operations Director, Sean oversees the day-to-day functions of the program, ensuring every client receives individualized, high-quality care. His leadership reflects his belief that treatment should honor the humanity of every person who walks through the door—whether it's their first time seeking help or they're returning after a relapse.

Professional Background

Sean brings more than three decades of personal recovery experience and professional leadership to Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Throughout his career, Sean has worked in various capacities within the addiction treatment industry, always guided by the belief that people deserve to be treated as individuals—not diagnoses.

He has seen firsthand how the right treatment environment can change lives. He has also witnessed the damage caused by programs that lack compassion, fail to offer individualized care, or treat clients as statistics rather than human beings.

Those experiences motivated him to create a treatment center that operates differently—one where clients are welcomed without judgment, supported with evidence-based care, and empowered to create lasting change.

Leadership Philosophy

Sean's leadership is guided by five core principles that shape every aspect of Desert Mountain Healing IOP:

1. Dignity

Every person who seeks treatment deserves to be treated with dignity. Addiction does not define a person's worth, and recovery begins when individuals are reminded of their inherent value.

2. Compassion

Sean believes compassion creates the foundation for healing. When people feel understood and supported, they are more likely to engage in treatment and commit to long-term recovery.

3. Respect

Respect means honoring each client's unique experiences, goals, and recovery journey. There is no "one-size-fits-all" approach to addiction treatment.

4. Hope

Hope is essential for recovery. Sean works to ensure every client knows that recovery is possible, no matter how many times they've tried before.

5. Accountability

While compassion is critical, accountability is equally important. Sean believes clients need supportive accountability to develop the skills, confidence, and resilience required for sustainable recovery.

Why Sean Built Desert Mountain Healing IOP

Sean co-founded Desert Mountain Healing IOP because he believes people struggling with addiction deserve better than what many treatment centers offer.

He wanted to create a program where:

  • Clients are treated with dignity, not judgment
  • First-time treatment seekers feel welcomed and supported
  • People returning after relapse are met with compassion, not shame
  • Treatment is personalized to meet individual needs
  • Recovery is built on hope, accountability, and evidence-based care

That vision became Desert Mountain Healing IOP—a place where recovery is possible, and every person is valued.

Sean's Role at Desert Mountain Healing IOP

As Co-Founder and Operations Director, Sean's responsibilities include:

  • Operational Leadership: Overseeing daily operations to ensure the program runs smoothly and efficiently
  • Quality Assurance: Ensuring every client receives high-quality, individualized care
  • Staff Development: Supporting and mentoring the treatment team to maintain the highest standards of compassionate care
  • Client Advocacy: Ensuring clients' voices are heard and their needs are prioritized
  • Community Engagement: Building relationships with referral sources, community organizations, and recovery advocates
  • Strategic Planning: Guiding the long-term vision and growth of Desert Mountain Healing IOP

Sean's leadership ensures that Desert Mountain Healing IOP remains true to its founding principles while continually improving the quality of care provided to every client.

Personal Message from Sean
