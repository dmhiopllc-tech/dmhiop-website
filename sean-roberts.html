Meet Sean Roberts

A Lifetime Dedicated to Recovery

For Sean Roberts, recovery is not an abstract concept—it's a lived reality spanning nearly four decades. Sober since 1987, Sean's personal journey through addiction and into sustained wellness forms the foundation of his leadership at Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Sean doesn't approach treatment from behind a desk or through textbooks. He understands addiction from the inside out—the desperation, the shame, the false starts, and ultimately, the hard-won freedom that comes with genuine recovery. This lived experience informs every decision he makes as Co-Founder and Operations Director.

Clients and staff alike describe Sean as grounded, authentic, and deeply committed to creating a treatment environment built on respect rather than judgment. He knows firsthand that recovery is not a straight line, and he meets people exactly where they are—with compassion, honesty, and an unwavering belief in their capacity for change.

Professional Background

From Personal Recovery to Building Systems of Care

Sean's path into addiction treatment leadership began with his own recovery in 1987. After achieving sobriety, he realized his experiences—both the pain of active addiction and the hope of recovery—could serve a greater purpose. Rather than simply rebuilding his own life, he felt called to create systems that would help others do the same.

Over the years, Sean worked in various roles within the addiction treatment field, gaining insight into what works—and what doesn't—in helping people achieve lasting recovery. He saw programs that prioritized bureaucracy over compassion, facilities that treated clients as numbers rather than people, and systems that failed to recognize the inherent dignity of every individual seeking help.

In 2023, Sean partnered with Gary Gamboa to co-found Desert Mountain Healing IOP, bringing together their combined decades of recovery experience to create something different: a treatment center rooted in lived experience, clinical excellence, and genuine care.

Sean's Key Responsibilities

  • Overseeing daily operations and ensuring quality care delivery
  • Building and maintaining community partnerships and referral networks
  • Ensuring regulatory compliance and best practice standards
  • Supporting staff development and fostering a culture of accountability
  • Collaborating with clinical leadership to refine treatment programming
  • Managing budgets, contracts, and organizational growth
  • Serving as a visible presence and role model for clients in treatment

Sean's leadership style is hands-on and relational. He's not a distant administrator—he's actively involved in the life of the facility, regularly interacting with clients, supporting staff, and ensuring that Desert Mountain Healing IOP remains a place where people feel seen, heard, and valued.

Leadership Philosophy

Leading with Dignity and Lived Experience

Sean's leadership philosophy is simple but profound: Every person seeking treatment deserves to be treated with the same dignity and respect you would want for yourself or someone you love.

This isn't just a slogan—it's the operational principle that guides every aspect of Desert Mountain Healing IOP. From the moment a client walks through the door to the day they complete treatment and beyond, Sean ensures that respect, compassion, and accountability are woven into every interaction.

Core Leadership Values

  • Dignity: Recognizing the inherent worth of every individual, regardless of their past
  • Compassion: Understanding that addiction is a disease, not a moral failing
  • Respect: Treating clients as partners in their own recovery, not subjects to be managed
  • Hope: Believing in the possibility of change, even when clients don't believe in themselves
  • Accountability: Holding clients and staff to high standards while offering grace and support

Sean leads by example. He shows up every day with the same commitment to growth, honesty, and service that he asks of his clients and staff. His presence at the facility is steady and consistent, a reminder that recovery is not just possible—it's sustainable, and it's worth the work.

Why Sean Built Desert Mountain Healing

Creating the Treatment Center He Wished Had Existed

When Sean reflects on his own journey, he remembers the treatment experiences that didn't work—the places that felt cold, institutional, and disconnected from the reality of what it's like to struggle with addiction. He also remembers the moments that did make a difference: the counselor who saw his potential, the peer who believed in him, the moment he realized he wasn't beyond help.

Desert Mountain Healing IOP was built to be a place where those moments of connection, compassion, and hope are the norm—not the exception. Sean wanted to create a treatment center where:

  • Clients are treated as whole people, not just diagnoses or case numbers
  • Staff members in recovery are celebrated for their lived experience, not hidden
  • Treatment is flexible and individualized, not one-size-fits-all
  • Families are included and supported throughout the recovery process
  • Long-term recovery is the goal, not just short-term compliance
  • Accountability and compassion exist side by side

For Sean, Desert Mountain Healing IOP isn't just a business—it's a mission. Every policy, every program decision, and every interaction is guided by one question: Is this the kind of care I would have wanted when I was in treatment?

Sean's Role on the Recovery Team

Hands-On Leadership and Client Support

As Operations Director, Sean's responsibilities are broad and varied. On any given day, you might find him:

  • Meeting with new clients during intake to ensure they feel welcomed and understood
  • Sitting in on staff meetings to problem-solve operational challenges
  • Reviewing treatment protocols and policies to ensure they reflect best practices
  • Building relationships with community partners, referral sources, and regulatory agencies
  • Supporting clinical staff in managing complex cases
  • Checking in with clients one-on-one to see how they're progressing
  • Handling administrative tasks, budgets, and long-term planning

Sean's presence is felt throughout the facility. Clients often describe him as someone who "gets it"—someone who doesn't just understand addiction academically but knows it personally. His authenticity, humility, and genuine care make him a powerful role model for clients who are just beginning to believe that recovery is possible.

What Clients Can Expect

Working with Sean

When you work with Sean, whether directly or through the programs and systems he's built, you can expect:

  • Honesty: Sean doesn't sugarcoat the challenges of recovery, but he balances truth with hope
  • Respect: He treats every person—regardless of their past—with dignity and compassion
  • Accessibility: Sean maintains an open-door policy and makes himself available to clients and staff
  • Accountability: He holds people to high standards while offering support and grace along the way
  • Lived Experience: He speaks from personal experience and understands the recovery journey intimately
  • Encouragement: Sean celebrates progress and reminds clients of how far they've come, especially on difficult days

Sean's leadership creates a culture where recovery is not just possible—it's expected. And when clients see his example, they begin to believe that they, too, can build a life worth living.

Why Sean Chose This Field

Turning Lived Experience into Purposeful Service

Sean didn't plan to work in addiction treatment—he didn't have a childhood dream of running a recovery center. But after experiencing the devastating impact of addiction and the transformative power of recovery, he felt called to give back.

"I know what it's like to feel hopeless," Sean says. "I know what it's like to think you'll never get better, that you're too broken to fix. And I also know what it's like to wake up one day and realize you're living a life you never thought possible. If I can help even one person get to that place, then all of this is worth it."

Sean chose this field because he believes in second chances—and third chances, and fourth chances. He knows that recovery isn't linear, and he's committed to walking alongside clients through every setback and every victory.

His nearly four decades in recovery are a testament to the fact that lasting change is possible. And every day, he shows up at Desert Mountain Healing IOP to prove it—not with words, but with action, presence, and unwavering belief in the capacity of every person to heal.

A Personal Message from Sean

You Don't Have to Do This Alone

"If you're reading this, you might be struggling. You might be scared, tired, or unsure if recovery is even possible for you. I want you to know: it is. I've been where you are, and I've seen thousands of people walk the path from addiction to recovery. It's not easy, but it's worth it. And you don't have to do it alone.

At Desert Mountain Healing, we don't just treat addiction—we help people rebuild their lives. We meet you where you are, without judgment, and walk with you every step of the way. Whether this is your first time seeking help or your tenth, we're here. And we believe in you, even if you don't believe in yourself yet.

Recovery is possible. Hope is real. And a better life is waiting for you. Let us help you find it."

— Sean Roberts
Co-Founder & Operations Director
