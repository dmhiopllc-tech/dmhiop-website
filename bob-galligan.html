Meet Bob

Three Decades of Clinical Excellence

For more than three decades, Robert "Bob" Galligan has dedicated his career to helping individuals and families overcome addiction, mental health challenges, and life's most difficult obstacles.

As Clinical Director of Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Bob provides the clinical leadership that ensures every client receives individualized, evidence-based treatment rooted in compassion, professionalism, and respect.

Bob believes recovery is never a one-size-fits-all process. Every individual arrives with a unique story, different strengths, and personal challenges. His philosophy centers on developing treatment plans that recognize the whole person—not simply the addiction—while helping clients build the skills, confidence, and resilience needed for lifelong recovery.

His calm, approachable style and extensive clinical experience have earned the trust of clients, families, healthcare professionals, and community partners throughout New Mexico.

Professional Background

Education and Clinical Credentials

Bob holds a Master of Education (M.Ed.) from Harvard University with a specialization in counseling psychology, giving him a strong foundation in the science of human behavior, therapeutic relationship-building, and evidence-based treatment methodologies.

He is a Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor (LPCC) and a Licensed Alcohol and Drug Abuse Counselor (LADAC) in New Mexico, credentials that reflect his commitment to maintaining the highest professional standards in addiction treatment and behavioral health.

Career Highlights

  • Over 30 years of experience in addiction treatment, mental health counseling, and clinical program leadership
  • Direct clinical experience working with individuals, families, and groups across all levels of care
  • Extensive expertise in evidence-based modalities including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Motivational Interviewing (MI), and trauma-informed care
  • Leadership roles in both nonprofit and private treatment settings
  • Ongoing professional development and continuing education in emerging treatment approaches

Bob's educational background and clinical credentials position him as one of the most respected and experienced treatment professionals in the Rio Rancho and Albuquerque areas.

Clinical Philosophy

Person-Centered, Evidence-Based Care

Bob's clinical philosophy is built on a foundation of respect, empathy, and evidence-based practice. He believes that effective treatment requires more than clinical expertise—it requires genuine human connection, cultural humility, and a commitment to meeting clients where they are.

Core Treatment Principles

  • Individualized Care: No two clients are the same, and treatment plans must reflect each person's unique needs, strengths, and goals
  • Whole-Person Approach: Addressing not just the addiction, but the underlying mental health, trauma, and life circumstances that contribute to it
  • Evidence-Based Modalities: Utilizing proven therapeutic approaches including CBT, MI, trauma-informed care, and family systems therapy
  • Collaboration and Empowerment: Clients are active participants in their own recovery, not passive recipients of treatment
  • Cultural Competence: Respecting and honoring the diverse backgrounds, values, and lived experiences of every client

Bob's approach ensures that every client at Desert Mountain Healing IOP receives treatment that is not only clinically sound, but also deeply respectful of their dignity, autonomy, and personal journey.

Role at Desert Mountain Healing

Clinical Director Responsibilities

As Clinical Director, Bob oversees all aspects of clinical programming at Desert Mountain Healing IOP. His leadership ensures that the treatment team delivers high-quality, evidence-based care that meets or exceeds state and federal standards.

Key Responsibilities

  • Providing clinical supervision and support to the treatment team
  • Developing and refining evidence-based treatment protocols and curricula
  • Conducting comprehensive assessments and individualized treatment planning
  • Overseeing quality assurance and clinical outcomes measurement
  • Ensuring compliance with state licensing requirements and best practice standards
  • Collaborating with external providers, referral sources, and community partners
  • Supporting staff training, professional development, and clinical consultation
  • Maintaining a culture of clinical excellence, ethical practice, and continuous improvement

Bob's leadership ensures that Desert Mountain Healing maintains its reputation for delivering compassionate, clinically rigorous treatment that changes lives.

Therapeutic Approach

Evidence-Based Modalities

Bob is trained and experienced in a wide range of evidence-based therapeutic modalities, allowing him to tailor treatment to each client's unique needs and preferences.

Key Treatment Modalities

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Helping clients identify and change unhelpful thinking patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI): Supporting clients in exploring and resolving ambivalence about change
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Recognizing and addressing the impact of trauma on addiction and recovery
  • Family Systems Therapy: Involving family members in the healing process and addressing relational dynamics
  • Mindfulness-Based Approaches: Integrating mindfulness and stress reduction techniques into treatment
  • Relapse Prevention: Building skills and strategies for maintaining long-term recovery

By integrating multiple therapeutic approaches, Bob ensures that clients receive comprehensive, holistic care that addresses all dimensions of their recovery journey.

Beyond the Clinic

Community Involvement and Advocacy

Bob is deeply committed to improving access to quality addiction treatment and reducing stigma in the broader community. He regularly collaborates with other treatment providers, healthcare professionals, and community organizations to strengthen the continuum of care in New Mexico.

He is a frequent speaker at professional conferences, treatment provider meetings, and community education events, sharing his expertise on evidence-based treatment, trauma-informed care, and effective clinical supervision.

Bob also serves as a mentor to early-career counselors and therapists, providing clinical supervision, professional guidance, and support as they develop their skills and advance their careers in the behavioral health field.

Outside of Work

Personal Interests and Wellness

Outside of his professional work, Bob is an avid reader with a particular interest in psychology, philosophy, and human resilience. He believes in the importance of lifelong learning and regularly explores new research and emerging trends in behavioral health and addiction treatment.

Bob enjoys spending time outdoors, hiking the trails around Rio Rancho and Albuquerque, and appreciating the natural beauty of New Mexico. He values time with family and friends and is committed to maintaining his own wellness through regular exercise, mindfulness practice, and community connection.

His personal commitment to balance, self-care, and continuous growth reflects the same values he encourages in his clients—that recovery and wellness are lifelong journeys, not destinations.

Why Bob Joined Desert Mountain Healing

A Shared Vision for Compassionate, Evidence-Based Care

Bob was drawn to Desert Mountain Healing IOP because of its commitment to providing high-quality, individualized treatment in a welcoming, nonjudgmental environment. He appreciated the founders' lived experience with recovery and their dedication to creating a treatment center that truly puts clients first.

For Bob, joining DMHIOP was an opportunity to bring his decades of clinical experience to a program that shares his values of compassion, respect, evidence-based practice, and whole-person care.

He's proud to be part of a team that believes recovery is possible for everyone—and that's dedicated to walking alongside clients every step of the way.
