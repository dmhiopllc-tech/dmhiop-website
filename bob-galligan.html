Meet Bob

Three Decades of Clinical Excellence

For more than three decades, Robert "Bob" Galligan has dedicated his career to helping individuals and families overcome addiction, mental health challenges, and life's most difficult obstacles.

As Clinical Director of Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Bob provides the clinical leadership that ensures every client receives individualized, evidence-based treatment rooted in compassion, professionalism, and respect.

Bob believes recovery is never a one-size-fits-all process. Every individual arrives with a unique story, different strengths, and personal challenges. His philosophy centers on developing treatment plans that recognize the whole person—not simply the addiction—while helping clients build the skills, confidence, and resilience needed for lifelong recovery.

His calm, approachable style and extensive clinical experience have earned the trust of clients, families, healthcare professionals, and community partners throughout New Mexico.

Professional Background

A Career Devoted to Healing

Bob has spent his professional career working with individuals experiencing substance use disorders, co-occurring mental health conditions, trauma, and complex life circumstances.

Throughout his career, he has provided clinical supervision, therapist mentoring, individual counseling, family counseling, crisis intervention, and program development across multiple levels of care.

His extensive experience allows him to guide both clients and clinicians through challenging situations while maintaining a focus on evidence-based practices and compassionate care.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Bob oversees clinical services, ensuring every member of the treatment team shares a common commitment to excellence, ethics, and individualized treatment.

Clinical Philosophy

Three Essential Foundations for Recovery

Bob believes successful recovery is built upon three essential foundations:

1. Every person deserves to be heard.

Healing begins when clients feel safe enough to tell their story without fear of judgment.

2. Recovery requires individualized care.

No two recovery journeys are identical. Treatment should always reflect each person's unique experiences, strengths, goals, and needs.

3. Growth happens through connection.

Strong therapeutic relationships, family support, peer encouragement, and community involvement all play critical roles in sustaining long-term recovery.

What Clients Can Expect

Working with Bob

Clients working with Bob can expect:

  • Respect and compassion in every interaction
  • Honest, collaborative communication throughout treatment
  • Evidence-based treatment recommendations tailored to individual needs
  • Individualized care that honors each person's unique journey

Why Bob Chose Behavioral Health

A Lifelong Calling to Help Others Heal

Bob has devoted his career to behavioral health because he believes people are capable of remarkable change when they are given the right support, guidance, and opportunity.

He considers it a privilege to walk alongside individuals during some of the most challenging—and ultimately rewarding—chapters of their lives.

Watching clients regain hope, reconnect with their families, and rebuild meaningful lives continues to inspire his work every day.

Working With the Treatment Team

Collaborative, Multidisciplinary Care

Bob believes exceptional treatment happens through collaboration.

He works closely with Desert Mountain Healing IOP's counselors, medical providers, peer support specialists, and case managers to ensure every client benefits from a coordinated, multidisciplinary approach.

This team-based philosophy allows clients to receive comprehensive care that addresses the medical, emotional, psychological, social, and practical aspects of recovery.
