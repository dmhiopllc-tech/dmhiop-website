Meet Alicia

Compassionate Support for Lasting Change

Alicia Chavez is a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome substance use disorders and develop the skills needed to achieve lasting recovery.

She believes that every person's recovery journey is unique, and effective treatment begins by listening without judgment, understanding each individual's experiences, and building a therapeutic relationship founded on trust, compassion, and respect.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Alicia provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, and works collaboratively with the multidisciplinary treatment team to help clients overcome barriers, strengthen coping skills, and build healthier, more meaningful lives.

Her compassionate and encouraging approach helps clients recognize their strengths while empowering them to take ownership of their recovery journey.

Professional Background

Experience in Addiction Counseling

Alicia has experience working with individuals facing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, life transitions, and other behavioral health challenges.

She believes meaningful recovery requires more than abstinence. It involves developing healthy relationships, improving emotional wellness, strengthening communication, and learning practical strategies for navigating life's challenges without returning to substance use.

As a counselor, Alicia is committed to helping clients discover their resilience and develop the confidence needed to create lasting change. She works alongside each individual to identify their strengths, address obstacles, and develop realistic goals that support lifelong recovery.

Her collaborative style encourages clients to actively participate in their treatment while celebrating progress and learning from setbacks.

Therapeutic Approach

Client-Centered, Strengths-Based Care

Alicia's therapeutic approach is rooted in empathy, respect, and the belief that every individual has the capacity to heal and grow.

Treatment Philosophy

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Helping clients identify and change unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI): Supporting clients in exploring their own reasons for change and building motivation
  • Strengths-Based Therapy: Focusing on client strengths, resilience, and resources
  • Relapse Prevention: Building practical skills and strategies for maintaining long-term recovery
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Creating a safe, supportive environment that recognizes the impact of trauma

Alicia believes that successful recovery happens when clients feel truly heard, respected, and empowered to make positive changes in their lives.

Areas of Focus

Supporting Recovery Across Multiple Challenges

Alicia provides counseling and support for individuals facing:

  • Substance use disorders (alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis)
  • Co-occurring mental health conditions (anxiety, depression)
  • Trauma and adverse life experiences
  • Life transitions and adjustment challenges
  • Relationship and family conflict
  • Emotional regulation and stress management
  • Relapse prevention and recovery maintenance
  • Building healthy coping skills and self-care practices

Her compassionate, nonjudgmental approach helps clients feel safe exploring difficult issues while working toward meaningful, sustainable change.

Group Therapy

Building Community and Connection

In addition to individual counseling, Alicia facilitates group therapy sessions where clients can connect with others who share similar experiences and challenges.

Benefits of Group Therapy

  • Reduces isolation and helps clients realize they're not alone
  • Provides peer support, encouragement, and accountability
  • Offers opportunities to practice communication and interpersonal skills
  • Reinforces recovery concepts through shared learning
  • Builds a sense of community and belonging

Alicia creates a safe, respectful group environment where clients feel comfortable sharing their experiences and supporting one another's recovery journeys.

Why Alicia Joined Desert Mountain Healing

Alicia was drawn to Desert Mountain Healing IOP because of its commitment to providing individualized, compassionate treatment that truly meets clients where they are.
