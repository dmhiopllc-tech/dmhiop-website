Meet Illiana

Building Hope, Creating Change

Illiana Craig is a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is dedicated to helping individuals overcome substance use disorders and build healthier, more fulfilling lives.

She believes every client deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, and compassion. Illiana understands that seeking treatment can be one of the most difficult decisions a person will ever make, and she strives to create a welcoming, supportive environment where clients feel safe to share their experiences without fear of judgment.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Illiana provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, and works collaboratively with the treatment team to help clients develop the tools, confidence, and resilience needed for long-term recovery.

Her caring and encouraging approach helps clients recognize their strengths while empowering them to create meaningful and lasting change.

Professional Background

Experience in Recovery & Behavioral Health

Illiana has experience working with individuals experiencing substance use disorders, emotional challenges, life transitions, and co-occurring behavioral health concerns.

She believes recovery involves more than achieving sobriety. It includes improving emotional wellness, strengthening relationships, building healthy coping skills, and creating a life filled with purpose and stability.

As a therapist, Illiana is committed to helping clients identify obstacles, develop practical solutions, and recognize the strengths that will support lifelong recovery.

She works closely with the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP to ensure every client receives individualized, comprehensive care.

Illiana earned her Bachelor's Degree from the University of New Mexico. She believes continuing education allows her to better serve clients by expanding her knowledge of addiction treatment, behavioral health, and evidence-based counseling practices.

Her commitment to lifelong learning reflects her dedication to providing compassionate, high-quality care while continually growing as a behavioral health professional.

Counseling Philosophy

Empowering Change Through Compassion

Illiana believes every individual possesses the ability to create positive change.

Her counseling philosophy is centered on four guiding principles:

1. Compassion Creates Trust

Healing begins when people feel accepted, respected, and understood.

2. Recovery Is Personal

Every individual's experiences, goals, and recovery journey are unique and deserve individualized care.

3. Growth Happens Through Support

Recovery becomes stronger when clients are surrounded by encouragement, accountability, and healthy relationships.

4. Small Steps Lead to Lasting Change

Meaningful recovery is achieved through consistent progress, one day and one decision at a time.
