Personalized Support for Your Journey

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, we're a treatment center founded by people in recovery, for people seeking recovery. We understand that healing isn't one-size-fits-all—that's why we offer individual counseling as a cornerstone of our comprehensive care approach.

Our licensed therapists provide evidence-based, compassionate one-on-one support because we believe every person deserves personalized attention on their path to wellness. Whether you're currently in our IOP or outpatient program, or you've completed treatment and need ongoing support, we're here for you.

We do this work because we've been where you are. Recovery is possible, and having someone in your corner who truly understands can make all the difference. That's the kind of support we provide—professional, personal, and proven to help.

Benefits of Individual Counseling

🎯

Personalized Treatment Plans

Every session is tailored specifically to your goals, challenges, and progress. Your therapist works with you to create a customized roadmap for your recovery journey.

🔒

Safe, Confidential Space

Private one-on-one sessions provide a judgment-free environment where you can openly discuss your thoughts, feelings, and concerns with complete confidentiality.

🧠

Evidence-Based Techniques

Our therapists use proven therapeutic approaches including CBT, DBT, motivational interviewing, and trauma-informed care to support your healing.

⚡

Flexible Scheduling

Sessions scheduled around your life—whether you're in active treatment or need ongoing support after program completion. We work with your schedule.

💪

Build Coping Skills

Develop practical tools and strategies to manage triggers, cravings, stress, and challenging emotions in healthy, sustainable ways.

🌱

Long-Term Support

Recovery doesn't end when treatment does. Continue working with your therapist for months or years after completing our programs—we're here for the long haul.

Who Can Benefit from Individual Counseling?

👥

Current IOP Clients

Supplement your group therapy with personalized one-on-one sessions to address specific issues and accelerate your progress.

🚶

Outpatient Clients

Receive dedicated individual support as part of your outpatient treatment plan, with sessions tailored to your schedule and needs.

🎓

Program Alumni

Continue your growth after completing treatment. Maintain momentum, navigate life's challenges, and prevent relapse with ongoing counseling support.

How Individual Counseling Works

1

Initial Assessment

Your first session focuses on understanding your history, current situation, goals, and challenges. Your therapist will work with you to develop a personalized treatment approach that fits your unique needs.

2

Regular Sessions

Meet with your dedicated therapist weekly, bi-weekly, or at a frequency that works for you. Sessions typically last 50-60 minutes and provide focused time to work through challenges and build skills.

3

Skill Development

Learn and practice evidence-based coping strategies, relapse prevention techniques, emotional regulation skills, and healthy communication patterns that support long-term recovery.

4

Ongoing Support

As you progress, your sessions evolve to meet your changing needs. Continue as long as you find value—whether that's weeks, months, or years. We're committed to your long-term success.

Ready to Take the Next Step?

Individual counseling can be a powerful tool in your recovery journey. Our licensed therapists are here to support you with compassion, expertise, and personalized care.
