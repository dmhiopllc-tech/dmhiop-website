📋 Program Basics

What is Desert Mountain Healing IOP?

Desert Mountain Healing IOP is an intensive outpatient addiction treatment program in Rio Rancho, NM, serving the greater Albuquerque metropolitan area. We provide structured, evidence-based treatment for individuals struggling with substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions.

Our outpatient program is designed to help you regain control of your life while remaining connected to your family, work, and community.

What makes your program different from others?

Our program is a top choice for addiction treatment because we offer:

  • In-Person & Virtual Treatment – In-person IOP in Rio Rancho and Virtual IOP options for eligible clients throughout New Mexico
  • Dual Diagnosis Care – We treat both addiction and mental health conditions simultaneously for lasting recovery
  • Flexible Scheduling – Morning and evening IOP options designed to fit your life, allowing you to maintain daily responsibilities
  • Experienced, Compassionate Team – Our licensed professionals are dedicated to your recovery journey
  • Whole-Person Approach – We address medical needs, emotional health, practical barriers, and long-term stability
  • Exclusive Recovery Portal – Custom technology extends engagement, goals, resources, and recovery activities beyond sessions
What is an Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)?

An IOP is a structured treatment program that allows individuals to receive comprehensive therapy and support while living at home. Unlike residential treatment, you can continue working, caring for family, and maintaining daily responsibilities.

Our IOP includes:

  • Group counseling and recovery education
  • Individual therapy and treatment planning
  • Relapse prevention strategies
  • Life skills and coping techniques
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) when appropriate
  • Case management for practical support

Learn more about IOP benefits

Who is IOP treatment designed for?

IOP is designed for adults who:

  • Need structured addiction treatment but can live at home safely
  • Have completed residential treatment and need ongoing support
  • Can manage withdrawal symptoms in an outpatient setting
  • Have work, family, or educational commitments they need to maintain
  • Are motivated to participate actively in their recovery
  • Have a stable, supportive living environment

An individualized assessment helps determine whether IOP is the right level of care for your situation.

💊 Treatment Details

How long does the IOP program last?

Desert Mountain Healing IOP is a structured 90-day intensive outpatient program. While the program is designed around a 90-day course of care, each client's treatment plan and attendance schedule are individualized according to clinical needs.

Attendance and treatment planning may take into account clinical recommendations, progress toward recovery goals, medical necessity, behavioral health stability, insurance authorization and coverage, and individual support needs.

Clients may be scheduled to attend up to five days per week. After completing IOP, many clients step down to outpatient counseling, continuing care, medication management, or other appropriate recovery supports.

What therapies are included in the program?

We offer a variety of evidence-based therapies, including:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) – Identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI) – Building internal motivation and commitment to change
  • Group Therapy & Peer Support – Developing connection, accountability, and shared recovery experiences
  • Relapse Prevention & Life Skills – Learning practical coping strategies for long-term sobriety
  • Trauma-Informed Care – Addressing underlying trauma and co-occurring mental health conditions
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) – When clinically appropriate for opioid or alcohol use disorders
Do you treat co-occurring mental health conditions?

Yes! We provide dual diagnosis care for clients struggling with both substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions such as:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety disorders
  • PTSD and trauma-related conditions
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Personality disorders

Treating both conditions simultaneously leads to better long-term outcomes and reduces the risk of relapse.

What is the daily schedule like?

We offer both morning and evening IOP programming Monday-Friday to help clients balance treatment with work, family, and other responsibilities.

  • Morning IOP: 9:00 AM-12:00 PM, Monday-Friday
  • Evening IOP: 5:00 PM-8:00 PM, Monday-Friday
  • Individual counseling, case management, MAT, and other services: Scheduled according to each client's individualized treatment plan

Clients may be scheduled to attend up to five days per week, depending on clinical needs and their individualized treatment plan.

Do you offer Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)?

Yes! We provide Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) for individuals with opioid or alcohol use disorders when clinically appropriate.

MAT combines FDA-approved medications (such as buprenorphine, naltrexone, or methadone) with counseling and behavioral therapies. This integrated approach:

  • Reduces cravings and withdrawal symptoms
  • Increases treatment retention and success rates
  • Decreases the risk of relapse and overdose
  • Improves overall quality of life and daily functioning

Learn more about our MAT program

Is family involvement encouraged?

Absolutely! Family support can play an important role in long-term recovery. We offer:

  • Family education sessions to help loved ones understand addiction and recovery
  • Communication skills training to rebuild trust and healthy relationships
  • Family therapy when appropriate and beneficial to your treatment goals
  • Support resources for family members affected by a loved one's substance use

While family involvement is encouraged, it's not required. Your treatment plan will be tailored to your individual needs and circumstances.

🚀 Getting Started

How do I get started with treatment?

Starting treatment is simple:

Step 1: Contact Us
Call (505) 270-0840, fill out our contact form, or verify your insurance online.

Step 2: Complete an Assessment
We'll schedule a confidential assessment to understand your needs, goals, and the best level of care for your situation.

Step 3: Verify Insurance & Finalize Admission
Our team will verify your insurance benefits, answer any questions, and help you schedule your first session.

Step 4: Begin Your Recovery Journey
Attend your first IOP session and start building the foundation for lasting recovery.

Can I start treatment immediately?

In many cases, yes! We understand that waiting for treatment can be difficult, and we work to minimize barriers to care.

Once your insurance is verified (or payment arrangements are made) and you've completed an initial assessment, we can often schedule your first IOP session within a few days.

Call us at (505) 270-0840 to discuss same-day or next-day admissions if you need to start treatment urgently.

Is treatment confidential?

Yes. Your privacy is protected by federal and state confidentiality laws, including:

  • HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
  • 42 CFR Part 2 (Federal regulations protecting substance use treatment records)

We do not disclose any information about your treatment without your written consent, except in specific circumstances required by law (such as imminent danger to yourself or others).

All client records, communications, and treatment information are kept strictly confidential.

What should I bring to my first session?

For your first IOP session, please bring:

  • Photo ID (driver's license or state ID)
  • Insurance card (if applicable)
  • Any relevant medical records or medication lists
  • Completed intake paperwork (if provided ahead of time)
  • An open mind and willingness to participate

You don't need to bring anything else. Just come ready to take the first step toward recovery.

What if I relapse during or after treatment?

Relapse is sometimes part of the recovery journey—and it doesn't mean you've failed. If you experience a setback:

  • Reach out immediately – Contact our team right away so we can provide support and adjust your treatment plan
  • Re-engage with treatment – We welcome clients back without judgment and work with you to identify what led to the relapse
  • Learn and grow – Each setback is an opportunity to strengthen coping skills and build resilience

Our goal is to support you through every stage of recovery—including challenges. You're not alone, and help is always available.

Do you offer continuing care after IOP?

Yes! We believe recovery is a lifelong journey, and ongoing support is essential for lasting success. After completing IOP, we offer:

  • Step-down outpatient counseling for continued therapy and relapse prevention
  • Medication management for clients on MAT
  • Alumni support groups and peer connection opportunities
  • Case management to help with employment, housing, benefits, and other practical needs
  • Referrals to community resources like 12-step programs, SMART Recovery, and peer support networks

We're here for you long after you complete our program.
