📋 Program Basics

What is Desert Mountain Healing IOP?

Desert Mountain Healing IOP is an intensive outpatient addiction treatment program in Rio Rancho, NM, serving the greater Albuquerque metropolitan area. We provide structured, evidence-based treatment for individuals struggling with substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions.

Our outpatient program is designed to help you regain control of your life while remaining connected to your family, work, and community.

Where is Desert Mountain Healing IOP located?

We are conveniently located at:

Rio Rancho, NM 87124

We proudly serve the entire Albuquerque metro area, including Rio Rancho, Corrales, Bernalillo, and surrounding New Mexico communities. Call us at (505) 270-0840 for directions or to schedule a visit.

What makes your program different from others?

Our program is a top choice for addiction treatment because we offer:

  • In-Person Treatment – Receive real support and accountability in a dedicated, healing environment (not virtual sessions)
  • Dual Diagnosis Care – We treat both addiction and mental health conditions simultaneously for lasting recovery
  • Flexible Scheduling – Morning and evening IOP options designed to fit your life, allowing you to maintain daily responsibilities
  • Experienced, Compassionate Team – Our licensed professionals are dedicated to your recovery journey
  • Whole-Person Approach – We address medical needs, emotional health, practical barriers, and long-term stability
  • Exclusive Recovery Portal – Custom technology extends engagement, goals, resources, and recovery activities beyond sessions
What is an Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)?

An IOP is a structured treatment program that allows individuals to receive comprehensive therapy and support while living at home. Unlike residential treatment, you can continue working, caring for family, and maintaining daily responsibilities.

Our IOP includes:

  • Group counseling and recovery education
  • Individual therapy and treatment planning
  • Relapse prevention strategies
  • Life skills and coping techniques
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) when appropriate
  • Case management for practical support

Learn more about IOP benefits

Who is IOP treatment designed for?

IOP is designed for adults who:

  • Need structured addiction treatment but can live at home safely
  • Have completed residential treatment and need ongoing support
  • Can manage withdrawal symptoms in an outpatient setting
  • Have work, family, or educational commitments they need to maintain
  • Are motivated to participate actively in their recovery
  • Have a stable, supportive living environment

An individualized assessment helps determine whether IOP is the right level of care for your situation.

💊 Treatment Details

How long does the IOP program last?

The length of our IOP varies depending on individual needs and progress. Most participants attend for 8-12 weeks, with sessions multiple times per week.

Treatment duration is personalized based on:

  • Clinical assessment and recommendations
  • Progress toward recovery goals
  • Insurance authorization and coverage
  • Medical and behavioral health stability
  • Individual circumstances and support needs

After completing IOP, many clients step down to outpatient counseling, continuing care, or medication management for ongoing support.

What therapies are included in the program?

We offer a variety of evidence-based therapies, including:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) – Identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI) – Building internal motivation and commitment to change
  • Group Therapy & Peer Support – Developing connection, accountability, and shared recovery experiences
  • Relapse Prevention & Life Skills – Learning practical coping strategies for long-term sobriety
  • Trauma-Informed Care – Addressing underlying trauma and co-occurring mental health conditions
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) – When clinically appropriate for opioid or alcohol use disorders
Do you treat co-occurring mental health conditions?

Yes! We provide dual diagnosis care for clients struggling with both substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions such as:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety disorders
  • Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Other mood and behavioral health conditions

Treating both conditions simultaneously is essential for lasting recovery. Our integrated approach addresses the whole person, not just the addiction.

What is the schedule for IOP sessions?

We offer flexible morning and evening programs to accommodate work, school, and family responsibilities:

Morning Program: Ideal for clients who work evenings or have afternoon commitments

Evening Program: Perfect for clients who work or attend school during the day

Both programs run Monday through Friday and provide the same high-quality clinical care. Sessions typically include group therapy, individual counseling, and recovery education.

The specific schedule will be discussed during your assessment based on your needs and availability.

Do you offer family support?

Yes! Family involvement is encouraged, and we provide education and support resources to help loved ones understand addiction and recovery.

When appropriate, families can receive:

  • Education about addiction and recovery
  • Communication strategies and boundary-setting skills
  • Support for understanding the treatment process
  • Resources for family members' own well-being

Healthy family relationships can be a powerful source of support during recovery.

Can I work or go to school while in IOP?

Absolutely! One of the main benefits of IOP is that you can continue working, attending school, or caring for family while receiving treatment.

Our flexible morning and evening scheduling is specifically designed to help you balance recovery with your daily responsibilities. Many clients maintain employment, attend classes, or care for children throughout the program.

💳 Insurance & Admissions

Do you accept insurance?

Absolutely! We accept most major insurance plans, including:

  • Medicaid (New Mexico)
  • Commercial insurance providers
  • PPO and HMO plans
  • Employer-sponsored health insurance

Contact us at (505) 270-0840 or verify your insurance online to understand your coverage and benefits.

How can I verify my insurance coverage?

You can verify your insurance coverage in two ways:

1. Call us directly: (505) 270-0840
Our team will review your benefits and help you understand your coverage options.

2. Online verification: Complete our online insurance verification form
We'll contact you with details about your coverage, copays, deductibles, and out-of-pocket costs.

Important: Insurance verification does not guarantee payment or authorization, but it helps you understand your financial responsibility before beginning treatment.

What if I don't have insurance?

We want to help everyone who needs treatment, regardless of insurance status. Options include:

Recovery NM: A public charity created to help New Mexico residents access substance use treatment when insurance or personal finances do not cover the full cost. Scholarship availability depends on funding, eligibility, program requirements, and treatment-center participation.

Visit Recovery NM to learn more

Contact our team to discuss payment options, sliding-scale fees, or other financial assistance that may be available.

Is there a waitlist?

Availability depends on current demand, but we strive to offer prompt admissions for those who need treatment.

Contact us at (505) 270-0840 to check current openings and schedule an assessment. We understand that timing matters in recovery, and we work to connect you with care as quickly as possible.

How do I enroll in the program?

Enrollment is a simple process:

Step 1: Call us at (505) 270-0840 for a confidential consultation

Step 2: Complete an individualized assessment to determine the best treatment plan for your needs

Step 3: Verify insurance coverage and discuss financial arrangements

Step 4: Begin your journey to recovery with personalized support from our compassionate team

🚀 Getting Started

What should I bring to my first session?

Please bring:

  • ✔ A valid photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
  • ✔ Insurance information (insurance card and policy details)
  • ✔ A list of current medications (including dosage and prescribing doctor)
  • ✔ Any relevant medical or psychiatric records (if available)
  • ✔ An open mind and willingness to heal and grow

If you have questions about what to bring, our team will provide specific instructions when you schedule your first appointment.

What happens during the assessment?

The assessment is a confidential conversation designed to understand your needs and create an individualized treatment plan.

During the assessment, we'll discuss:

  • Your substance use history and current concerns
  • Medical and mental health history
  • Previous treatment experiences (if any)
  • Current life circumstances, responsibilities, and support system
  • Recovery goals and what you hope to achieve
  • Appropriate level of care and treatment recommendations

The assessment is not judgmental. Our goal is to understand your unique situation and provide the support you need.

What if I've tried treatment before and relapsed?

Relapse is a common part of recovery for many people, and you will be welcomed back without judgment.

Our team understands that recovery isn't always linear. What matters most is that you're taking the next right step forward. We'll help you:

  • Understand what happened and what you can learn from the experience
  • Strengthen your recovery plan with new strategies and support
  • Connect with appropriate ongoing care (IOP, MAT, counseling, case management)
  • Address any new or unmet needs that may have contributed to relapse

Every person's recovery journey is different, and we're here to support you wherever you are in that process.

Will my treatment information be kept confidential?

Yes. Your privacy is protected by federal law under HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and 42 CFR Part 2 (federal confidentiality regulations for substance use treatment).

We will not share your treatment information with anyone without your written consent, except in specific situations required by law (such as immediate safety concerns or court orders).

Your confidentiality and dignity are always respected at Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Can I visit the facility before enrolling?

Yes! We welcome prospective clients and their families to visit our facility and meet our team.

Call us at (505) 270-0840 to schedule a tour or to ask any questions about our program, environment, and treatment approach.

Seeing the facility in person can help you feel more comfortable and confident about taking the next step in your recovery journey.

How soon can I start treatment?

We understand that timing matters in recovery. In many cases, you can begin treatment within a few days of your initial contact, depending on:

  • Current program availability
  • Completion of the assessment process
  • Insurance verification and authorization
  • Your personal readiness and schedule

Call us today at (505) 270-0840 to get started as soon as possible.

Our philosophy

Treatment Built on Compassion and Respect

Desert Mountain Healing IOP was founded by Sean Roberts and Gary Gamboa because they believed addiction treatment could be delivered with greater compassion, integrity, accountability, and hope.

After experiencing recovery firsthand, they recognized that lasting recovery requires more than simply treating addiction. It requires hope, accountability, evidence-based care, and a treatment team that truly sees the person behind the diagnosis.

That belief became the foundation of Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Today, every client who walks through our doors is treated as an individual—not a number. We believe recovery is possible for everyone, and our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence-based treatment that empowers people to rebuild their lives.

You Will Be Welcomed Here

Whether you're seeking help for the first time or returning after a relapse, you will be welcomed without judgment.

Our team understands the courage it takes to ask for help. We know that recovery isn't linear, that setbacks happen, and that what matters most is taking the next right step forward.

Recovery begins with hope, grows through connection, and succeeds through compassionate care.
