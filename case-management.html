How Case Management Helps You

🗺️

Personalized Care Planning

We work with you to identify practical needs, priorities, and goals that complement your clinical treatment plan.

🔗

Resource Coordination

Explore referrals to housing, employment, transportation, childcare, legal aid, and other community resources based on availability and eligibility.

🏥

Healthcare Navigation

Get help communicating with healthcare providers and navigating referrals, appointments, and insurance questions when appropriate.

📋

Benefits Assistance

Receive guidance on locating application resources for Medicaid, SNAP, and other assistance programs for which you may qualify.

🛡️

Crisis Support

Identify appropriate crisis resources and referrals when urgent needs arise. For immediate danger, call 911; for a mental health crisis, call or text 988.

📞

Ongoing Advocacy

We support you in communicating your needs and preferences with providers and community agencies, with appropriate consent.

Who Benefits from Case Management?

👥

Current IOP Clients

Coordinate referrals and practical supports while participating in our Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP).

🌱

Transitioning Clients

Plan referrals and next steps when transitioning from IOP to outpatient care or another level of service.

🏠

Clients with Complex Needs

Clients managing housing instability, co-occurring concerns, legal requirements, or family needs may benefit from coordinated referrals.
