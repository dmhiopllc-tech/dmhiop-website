🤝 Case Management Services

Your dedicated advocate for navigating recovery, coordinating care, and accessing the resources you need to succeed

Your Personal Recovery Navigator

Recovery is a journey that often requires more than just treatment—it requires support in navigating complex systems, coordinating care, and accessing vital resources. Our professional case managers serve as your dedicated advocate, helping you overcome barriers and connect with the services you need to build a stable, healthy life.

Need help coordinating care?

We connect you with medical providers, specialists, and community resources.

Struggling with housing or employment?

We provide guidance and referrals to support your practical needs.

Overwhelmed by the system?

We advocate for you and help navigate insurance, benefits, and services.

Want ongoing support?

We stay with you throughout your recovery journey, adjusting as your needs change.

How Case Management Helps You

🗺️

Personalized Care Planning

We develop an individualized plan tailored to your unique needs, goals, and circumstances—addressing not just treatment, but all aspects of your recovery and well-being.

🔗

Resource Coordination

Connect with housing assistance, employment services, transportation, childcare, legal aid, and other community resources that support your recovery and stability.

🏥

Healthcare Navigation

Coordinate with medical providers, specialists, pharmacies, and insurance companies to ensure you receive comprehensive, integrated care without confusion or gaps.

📋

Benefits Assistance

Get help applying for Medicaid, disability benefits, food assistance (SNAP), and other programs you may be eligible for to support your basic needs during recovery.

🛡️

Crisis Support

Access immediate guidance and resources during difficult moments, including connections to crisis services, emergency housing, and urgent care coordination.

📞

Ongoing Advocacy

We speak up for your needs, rights, and preferences when dealing with systems, providers, or agencies—ensuring your voice is heard and respected.

Who Benefits from Case Management?

👥

Current IOP Clients

Receive comprehensive support while actively participating in our Intensive Outpatient Program to maximize your treatment outcomes and life stability.

🌱

Transitioning Clients

Get continued support when stepping down from IOP to outpatient care or transitioning to other levels of service—ensuring continuity of care.

🏠

Clients with Complex Needs

Those facing multiple challenges like housing instability, co-occurring disorders, legal issues, or family concerns benefit from coordinated, wraparound support.
