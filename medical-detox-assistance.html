Our Outpatient Detox Services

Our outpatient medical detox assistance is integrated into a comprehensive recovery program that begins immediately. Following an assessment by our Nurse Practitioner, each client receives an individualized plan designed to support physical health, emotional stability, and long-term recovery while the client continues living at home. Services may include:

  • Comprehensive Nurse Practitioner Assessment: Evaluation of withdrawal risk, medical and psychiatric needs, substance use history, home stability, available support, and whether outpatient care is appropriate
  • Outpatient Withdrawal Support: Monitoring, education, and medical support during withdrawal when outpatient care is clinically appropriate
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment: FDA-approved medications when clinically indicated to help manage withdrawal symptoms, reduce cravings, and support recovery
  • Individual Counseling: One-on-one work with licensed clinicians to address substance use, mental health needs, motivation, coping skills, and recovery goals
  • Group Therapy: Structured treatment that builds recovery skills, accountability, connection, and peer support from the beginning of care
  • Case Management: Practical assistance with barriers involving employment, housing, transportation, insurance, legal concerns, healthcare, and community resources
  • Family Education and Support: Guidance for loved ones when appropriate and authorized by the client
  • Recovery and Continuing-Care Planning: Relapse prevention, healthy routines, support connections, and a plan for continued treatment and long-term recovery

Is Outpatient Detox Right for You?

Our outpatient medical detox assistance program may be appropriate for individuals who meet the following criteria and are determined to be medically stable by our Nurse Practitioner. This is an outpatient program, which means clients do not stay overnight at our facility.

  • Are experiencing withdrawal symptoms that can be managed safely in an outpatient setting
  • Have a stable living environment and an appropriate support system
  • Are motivated to participate in treatment
  • Do not require 24-hour medical or psychiatric monitoring
  • Can attend regular treatment and medical appointments
  • Can safely follow the individualized outpatient treatment plan

Important safety information: Withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines, and certain other substances can become dangerous or life-threatening. If you are experiencing seizures, hallucinations, severe confusion, chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, thoughts of harming yourself or another person, or another medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Our Nurse Practitioner will assess whether outpatient detox support is safe and appropriate. When a person requires inpatient detoxification or 24-hour medical monitoring, we will help coordinate care with an appropriate facility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an inpatient detox facility?

No. Desert Mountain Healing IOP provides outpatient medical detox assistance. Clients attend scheduled appointments and return home. People who need 24-hour medical monitoring are referred to an appropriate inpatient or hospital-based setting.

How is outpatient eligibility determined?

A Nurse Practitioner completes an assessment that considers withdrawal risk, medical and psychiatric needs, substance use history, home stability, available support, and the person's ability to attend scheduled care.

What happens if outpatient detox is not appropriate?

Our team helps coordinate a referral to an appropriate level of care, which may include inpatient detoxification, a hospital, or another medically monitored program.

Does treatment begin after detox is completed?

No. At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, outpatient medical detox assistance is integrated into treatment. Clients begin participating in counseling, group therapy, case management, relapse prevention, and recovery planning immediately while receiving medical withdrawal support when appropriate.

Can I detox at home?

Some people can safely receive outpatient detox assistance while living at home, but this must be determined through an individualized medical assessment. Withdrawal risk, medical and psychiatric needs, substance use history, home stability, support, and the ability to attend treatment are all considered.

Is outpatient detox safe?

Outpatient detox assistance may be safe for people who are medically and clinically appropriate for this level of care. It is not appropriate for everyone. Our Nurse Practitioner assesses safety before outpatient services begin and recommends a higher level of care when necessary.

Will medication be provided?

Medication may be recommended when clinically appropriate. Decisions are based on the Nurse Practitioner's assessment, the substance involved, withdrawal symptoms, medical history, current medications, and the individualized treatment plan.

Can I continue working during outpatient treatment?

Some clients are able to continue working or managing family responsibilities while receiving outpatient care. This depends on withdrawal symptoms, safety, treatment recommendations, appointment schedules, and the person's individual circumstances.

Does insurance cover outpatient detox assistance?

Coverage varies by insurance plan and may depend on eligibility, medical necessity, authorization requirements, deductibles, copayments, and other plan provisions. Our office can help verify benefits before treatment begins, although verification is not a guarantee of payment.
