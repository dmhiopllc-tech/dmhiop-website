Meet Michelle

Hope, Connection, and Peer Support

Michelle Martin is a Certified Peer Support Worker (CPSW), Comprehensive Community Support Services (CCSS) Provider, and Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals discover hope, healing, and lasting recovery.

Michelle believes recovery is built through genuine human connection. She understands that meaningful change happens when people feel accepted, encouraged, and supported by someone who truly believes in their ability to succeed.

Her unique combination of peer support, community-based services, and clinical counseling allows her to provide compassionate, well-rounded care that addresses both the emotional and practical challenges individuals face during recovery.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Michelle provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, connects clients with community resources, and helps individuals build the confidence and life skills needed to sustain long-term recovery.

Professional Background

Clinical Counseling Meets Peer Support

Michelle has dedicated her career to helping individuals overcome substance use disorders while addressing the many life challenges that often accompany addiction.

Her experience as both a Certified Peer Support Worker and a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate gives her a unique perspective on recovery. She understands that healing involves much more than stopping substance use—it requires rebuilding confidence, strengthening relationships, improving mental wellness, and developing practical life skills.

Michelle believes recovery should empower individuals to become active participants in their own healing while helping them reconnect with their families, communities, and personal goals.

She works closely with the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP to ensure every client receives comprehensive, individualized care.

Peer Support Specialty

The Power of Shared Experience

As a Certified Peer Support Worker, Michelle brings a unique perspective to recovery. Peer support recognizes that individuals who have successfully navigated their own challenges can offer powerful encouragement, hope, and practical guidance to others facing similar struggles.

What Peer Support Offers

  • Lived Experience: Michelle understands recovery from a personal perspective, bringing authenticity and empathy to her work
  • Hope and Inspiration: Her own journey demonstrates that recovery is possible, even when things feel overwhelming
  • Practical Guidance: She helps clients navigate real-world challenges like housing, employment, family relationships, and community reintegration
  • Non-Judgmental Support: Peer support creates a safe space where clients feel understood and accepted
  • Connection and Belonging: Michelle helps clients build supportive relationships and reconnect with their communities

Michelle's peer support approach complements clinical treatment by addressing the day-to-day realities of recovery and helping clients develop the confidence to overcome obstacles.

Community Support Services

Connecting Clients with Resources

As a Comprehensive Community Support Services (CCSS) Provider, Michelle helps clients access the practical resources and community support systems they need to succeed in recovery.

Community Support Services

  • Connecting clients with housing resources and sober living options
  • Assisting with employment readiness and job search support
  • Linking clients to financial assistance programs and benefits
  • Providing transportation assistance and resource navigation
  • Supporting family reunification and relationship rebuilding
  • Connecting clients with community support groups and recovery networks
  • Helping clients access healthcare, mental health services, and social services

Michelle understands that successful recovery often depends on having stable housing, meaningful employment, supportive relationships, and access to community resources. She works diligently to ensure every client has the practical support they need to build a sustainable life in recovery.

Therapeutic Approach

Empowerment, Connection, and Practical Support

Michelle's therapeutic approach integrates evidence-based counseling with peer support and community-based services to provide holistic, empowering care.

Core Treatment Modalities

  • Motivational Interviewing (MI): Supporting clients in discovering their own motivations for change
  • Strengths-Based Therapy: Focusing on client strengths, resilience, and personal resources
  • Peer Support: Using shared experience to inspire hope and provide practical guidance
  • Community-Based Services: Connecting clients with resources that support long-term stability
  • Relapse Prevention: Building skills and strategies for maintaining recovery
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Creating a safe, supportive environment that recognizes the impact of trauma

Michelle believes that recovery is most successful when clients feel empowered, supported, and connected to their communities.

Areas of Focus

Comprehensive Recovery Support

Michelle provides counseling, peer support, and community services for individuals facing:

  • Substance use disorders (alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis)
  • Co-occurring mental health challenges (anxiety, depression)
  • Homelessness or housing instability
  • Unemployment and economic challenges
  • Family conflict and relationship difficulties
  • Social isolation and lack of community connection
  • Life transitions and reentry challenges
  • Relapse prevention and recovery maintenance

Her holistic approach addresses both the emotional and practical aspects of recovery, helping clients build sustainable, meaningful lives.
