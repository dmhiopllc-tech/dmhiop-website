Meet Michelle

Hope, Connection, and Peer Support

Michelle Martin is a Certified Peer Support Worker (CPSW), Comprehensive Community Support Services (CCSS) Provider, and Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals discover hope, healing, and lasting recovery.

Michelle believes recovery is built through genuine human connection. She understands that meaningful change happens when people feel accepted, encouraged, and supported by someone who truly believes in their ability to succeed.

Her unique combination of peer support, community-based services, and clinical counseling allows her to provide compassionate, well-rounded care that addresses both the emotional and practical challenges individuals face during recovery.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Michelle provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, connects clients with community resources, and helps individuals build the confidence and life skills needed to sustain long-term recovery.

Professional Background

Clinical Counseling Meets Peer Support

Michelle has dedicated her career to helping individuals overcome substance use disorders while addressing the many life challenges that often accompany addiction.

Her experience as both a Certified Peer Support Worker and a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate gives her a unique perspective on recovery. She understands that healing involves much more than stopping substance use—it requires rebuilding confidence, strengthening relationships, improving mental wellness, and developing practical life skills.

Michelle believes recovery should empower individuals to become active participants in their own healing while helping them reconnect with their families, communities, and personal goals.

She works closely with the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP to ensure every client receives comprehensive, individualized care.

Recovery & Counseling Philosophy

Three Essential Foundations

Michelle believes recovery is built on three essential foundations:

1. Hope

Every person deserves to believe that recovery is possible, regardless of their past.

2. Connection

Healing occurs through supportive relationships built on compassion, trust, and understanding.

3. Empowerment

Recovery is about helping people recognize their own strengths while providing the tools and encouragement needed to build independent, fulfilling lives.

Michelle believes lasting recovery happens when clients feel heard, respected, and supported throughout every stage of their journey.
