Skills for life beyond treatment

What You Will Work On in IOP

Treatment addresses substance use while also helping clients strengthen the emotional, physical, relational, and practical foundations of recovery.

Understanding Addiction

Recognize patterns, consequences, triggers, and the ways substance use affects the brain, body, and behavior.

Cravings and Triggers

Develop strategies for managing urges, high-risk situations, and early warning signs.

Emotional Intelligence

Identify emotions, communicate needs, and respond rather than react.

Distress Tolerance

Practice healthy ways to get through difficult moments without returning to substance use.

Reframing Thoughts

Challenge negative self-talk, unhelpful beliefs, and thinking patterns that can undermine recovery.

Confidence and Self-Worth

Rebuild trust in yourself through responsibility, consistency, healthy boundaries, and achievable goals.

Healthy Relationships

Improve communication, recognize unhealthy dynamics, make amends when appropriate, and build sober support.

Acceptance and Letting Go

Learn the difference between acceptance and approval while focusing energy on what you can change.

Purpose and Meaning

Explore values, strengths, interests, and goals that make recovery worth protecting.

Nutrition and Wellness

Understand how sleep, food, movement, medical care, and stress management support mental health and recovery.

Personal Responsibility

Take ownership of choices while replacing shame with honesty, accountability, and constructive action.

Long-Term Recovery Planning

Create a realistic plan for meetings, support, routines, goals, warning signs, and continued care.

Who may benefit

Is Intensive Outpatient Treatment Right for You?

IOP may be appropriate when someone needs more structure than standard outpatient care but does not require 24-hour residential treatment. The right level of care is determined through assessment.

You Need Structure and Accountability

You may benefit from frequent treatment, recovery education, drug testing, clinical support, and a consistent weekly routine.

You Need to Keep Daily Responsibilities

IOP can allow many people to continue living at home, working, attending school, parenting, and participating in family life.

You Are Stepping Down From Higher Care

IOP can provide a structured transition after detox, residential treatment, hospitalization, or another higher level of care.

Unsure which level of care is appropriate?

A confidential assessment can help identify the safest and most useful next step.

Common questions about intensive outpatient treatment

What to Expect

Understanding the program before you begin can make taking the first step feel more manageable.

An intensive outpatient program provides structured substance use treatment several days each week while allowing clients to continue living at home. It offers more support and frequency than standard outpatient care without requiring overnight residence.
Morning IOP is offered Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Evening IOP is offered Monday through Friday from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Call to confirm current availability.
Many clients continue working, attending school, parenting, or caring for family while participating. Morning and evening schedules can provide added flexibility.
Length of participation depends on clinical needs, progress, attendance, treatment goals, insurance authorization, and recommendations from the treatment team. Your plan is reviewed throughout care.
A typical day may include check-in, recovery education, group counseling, relapse-prevention work, coping-skills practice, discussion, and planning for how to apply skills outside treatment.
Individual counseling and other one-on-one services are provided according to each client's treatment plan, clinical needs, and level-of-care requirements.
Medical services may include medication-assisted treatment, medication management, and outpatient detox assistance when clinically appropriate and recommended by a qualified provider.
Case management can help clients identify barriers and connect with appropriate community resources, including employment, benefits, transportation, healthcare, identification, and education support.
With appropriate written authorization, our team can coordinate relevant treatment information with approved referral partners, courts, probation officers, or other providers.
Your Recovery Portal is a client engagement tool that may include daily check-ins, journaling, goals, meeting information, recovery resources, appointments, progress tracking, and other supports between sessions.
Clients may step down to outpatient services and continuing care based on clinical recommendations. The goal is to reduce intensity gradually while maintaining connection, accountability, and support.
Coverage varies by plan and service. Our team can help verify benefits and explain what information is available before admission.
Ask our team about payment options and possible Recovery NM scholarship assistance for eligible uninsured or underinsured New Mexico residents. Funding is not guaranteed and depends on eligibility and availability.
Timing depends on assessment availability, clinical appropriateness, insurance verification, and current program capacity. Calling is the fastest way to learn the next available step.
A comprehensive assessment helps determine the most appropriate level of care based on substance use, withdrawal risk, medical needs, mental health, safety, support, motivation, and recovery environment.
