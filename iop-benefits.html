Comprehensive outpatient care

Recovery Is Stronger When Every Part of Treatment Works Together

Addiction can affect physical health, emotional well-being, relationships, work, housing, finances, and daily stability. Effective treatment should address more than one part of that experience.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, clients receive coordinated support from a team that understands recovery personally and professionally. Services are selected according to each client’s assessment, health needs, responsibilities, and goals.

Clients can begin developing coping skills, receiving counseling, addressing practical barriers, and building a long-term recovery plan from the beginning of care.

Your recovery pathway

A Clear Plan from the First Call Forward

No two people enter treatment with the same needs. Some require medical stabilization, some need structured group treatment, and others need help rebuilding routines, employment, relationships, or community support.

Our team identifies the safest and most useful starting point, then adjusts the plan as the client progresses.

Important: This pathway is flexible. Services are recommended according to individualized medical and clinical assessment—not a one-size-fits-all sequence.

Core treatment services

Clinical and Medical Care for Every Stage of Recovery

Each service is designed to work as part of one coordinated plan, with recommendations based on individual needs and clinical appropriateness.

01

Intensive Outpatient Program

IOP provides a structured level of treatment while allowing clients to continue living at home and remain connected to family, work, school, or other responsibilities.

  • Morning and evening programming Monday through Friday
  • Group counseling and recovery education
  • Individual counseling and treatment planning
  • Relapse prevention, coping skills, and life skills
  • Ongoing assessment and step-down planning
Explore IOP benefits →
02

Medication-Assisted Treatment

Medication may be combined with counseling and behavioral health services to support recovery from opioid or alcohol use disorders when clinically appropriate.

  • Medical evaluation and medication management
  • Coordination with counseling and IOP services
  • Education about medication, recovery, and safety
  • Regular follow-up based on clinical need
  • Whole-person support rather than medication alone
Learn about MAT →
03

Outpatient Medical Detox Assistance

Some clients may be appropriate for outpatient medical support while managing withdrawal and beginning recovery services. Safety and appropriateness are determined through medical and clinical assessment.

  • Nurse Practitioner assessment
  • Withdrawal-symptom monitoring and support
  • Connection to counseling and recovery planning
  • Referral to a higher level of care when needed
  • Individualized follow-up and safety planning

Recovery NM

Financial Barriers Should Not Decide Who Gets a Chance at Recovery

Recovery NM is a public charity created to help New Mexico residents access substance use treatment when insurance or personal finances do not cover the full cost.

Scholarship availability depends on funding, eligibility, program requirements, and treatment-center participation. Recovery NM is separate from insurance verification and does not guarantee funding.

Common questions

Getting Started with Treatment

Can I continue working while attending treatment?

Many clients continue working, attending school, or caring for family while participating in outpatient treatment. Morning and evening options can make scheduling more manageable.

How do I know which services I need?

The recommended combination of services is based on an individualized assessment of clinical needs, health, goals, schedule, current risks, and available support.

Do you offer medication-assisted treatment?

Yes. When clinically appropriate, medication may be combined with counseling, structured outpatient care, case management, and continued recovery support.

Can you verify my insurance before I begin?

Yes. Our team can review available benefits and explain likely next steps. Insurance verification does not guarantee payment or authorization.

Is outpatient detox assistance safe for everyone?

No. Suitability depends on substance use, withdrawal risk, medical history, home support, and other factors. Some people require inpatient or hospital-based withdrawal management.

What happens after IOP?

Clients may step down to outpatient groups, individual counseling, medication follow-up, case management, peer support, and a continuing-care plan based on progress and need.
