What to Expect

Understanding the program before you begin can make taking the first step feel more manageable.

An intensive outpatient program provides structured addiction treatment several days each week while allowing clients to continue living at home.
Our AM IOP is offered Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Our PM IOP is offered Monday through Friday from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
Many clients continue working, attending school, or caring for family while participating. Morning and evening program options can provide additional flexibility.
Individual services are based on the client's treatment plan, clinical needs, and level-of-care requirements.
A comprehensive assessment helps determine the most appropriate level of care based on substance use, medical needs, mental health, safety, support, and recovery environment.
