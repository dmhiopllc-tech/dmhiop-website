Desert Mountain Healing IOP LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Desert Mountain Healing IOP LLC ("DMHIOP," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information through our website, inquiries, and administrative text messaging. It does not replace our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices or any notices or authorizations required for substance use disorder treatment records.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, information you submit through website forms or direct communications, appointment and administrative communication details, SMS consent and opt-out preferences, and basic website technical information such as browser information and server logs. Please do not submit sensitive medical or treatment details through general website contact forms or ordinary SMS.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, coordinate intake and appointments, communicate administrative information, support routine operations, maintain consent and opt-out records, operate and secure our website, and comply with applicable laws. We do not use consent to receive conversational SMS as permission to send marketing messages.

Who information may be shared with

We may share information with service providers that help operate our website, phone and messaging systems, and administrative services, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements. We may also disclose information when legally required or when otherwise permitted or authorized by applicable law. Protected health information and substance use disorder treatment records are subject to additional restrictions, including HIPAA and, where applicable, 42 CFR Part 2.

SMS privacy and consent

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell SMS opt-in information or mobile phone numbers for marketing. Limited information may be processed by messaging service providers solely to deliver and administer requested communications, subject to applicable safeguards. You may withdraw consent for text messages by replying STOP; for assistance, reply HELP or call 505-270-0840. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of receiving services.

Confidentiality and communication security

Standard SMS may not be secure. We ask that you avoid sending diagnoses, medication details, substance use history, or other sensitive treatment information by text. For confidential matters, call our office to discuss an appropriate communication method. Our handling of treatment information is also governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices and applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain information as needed for legitimate business purposes and applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements. We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information; however, no website or electronic transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional website information or to opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. To ask about your information or communication preferences, contact our office. Rights concerning treatment records are explained in our separate Notice of Privacy Practices.

Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy with an updated effective date.
