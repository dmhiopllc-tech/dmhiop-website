Meet Nicole

Discovering Strength, Building Hope

Nicole Clay is a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome substance use disorders and build healthier, more meaningful lives.

She believes every person deserves compassionate, individualized care that recognizes both the challenges they have faced and the strengths they already possess. Nicole strives to create a supportive environment where clients feel comfortable sharing their experiences, exploring personal growth, and developing the confidence needed for lasting recovery.

As a Primary Therapist at Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Nicole provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, and works collaboratively with the treatment team to develop personalized treatment plans that address the unique needs of each client.

Her warm, encouraging approach helps clients recognize that recovery is not simply about overcoming addiction—it's about creating a life filled with purpose, connection, and hope.

Professional Background

Experience in Addiction & Behavioral Health

Nicole has experience working with individuals experiencing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, and other behavioral health challenges.

She is committed to helping clients understand the underlying factors contributing to addiction while teaching practical skills that support long-term recovery and emotional wellness.

Nicole believes meaningful recovery involves developing healthy coping strategies, strengthening relationships, improving self-esteem, and building the confidence to overcome life's challenges without returning to substance use.

She works closely with the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP to ensure clients receive comprehensive, coordinated care throughout every stage of their recovery journey.

Future Educational Goals

Following completion of her bachelor's degree, Nicole plans to continue her education by entering a Master's degree program to further expand her clinical knowledge and better serve individuals and families affected by substance use and mental health disorders.

Nicole believes lifelong learning is essential to providing the highest quality behavioral healthcare and is committed to continually strengthening her clinical skills throughout her career.

Counseling Philosophy

Hope, Compassion, and Growth

Nicole believes recovery begins with hope and grows through compassion, accountability, and genuine human connection.

Her counseling philosophy is guided by four principles:

1. Every Person Has Strength

Every client has the ability to overcome adversity and create positive change.

2. Healing Happens Through Trust

Building a strong therapeutic relationship provides the foundation for lasting recovery.

3. Recovery Is About Building a Better Future

Recovery means creating a meaningful life filled with healthy relationships, purpose, and emotional wellness.

4. Learning Never Stops

Both clients and clinicians continue growing throughout life. Nicole believes continued education, self-reflection, and personal growth benefit everyone.
