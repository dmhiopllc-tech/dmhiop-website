Meet Regina

Building Trust, Fostering Healing

Regina Largent is a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome addiction, strengthen their mental health, and build fulfilling lives in recovery.

She believes that healing begins with trust. By creating a safe, supportive, and respectful therapeutic environment, Regina helps clients feel comfortable exploring the challenges that have contributed to substance use while discovering the strengths they already possess.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Regina provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, and works collaboratively with the treatment team to help clients develop the skills needed for long-term recovery.

Her compassionate approach encourages clients to recognize that recovery is not about perfection—it's about consistent progress, personal growth, and learning healthier ways to respond to life's challenges.

Professional Background

Experience in Behavioral Health & Recovery

Regina has dedicated her career to working with individuals experiencing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship difficulties, and other behavioral health challenges.

Her experience includes helping clients identify unhealthy thinking patterns, improve emotional regulation, strengthen communication skills, and develop practical coping strategies that support lasting recovery.

She understands that many individuals entering treatment have experienced significant pain, loss, or trauma, and she believes healing occurs through patience, understanding, accountability, and evidence-based therapeutic interventions.

As part of the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Regina works closely with medical providers, case managers, peer support specialists, and fellow therapists to ensure clients receive coordinated, comprehensive care.

Counseling Philosophy

Building Connection, Fostering Growth

Regina believes effective therapy is built upon genuine human connection.

Her counseling philosophy is guided by four principles:

1. Every Person Has Value

Each client deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion, and respect, regardless of their past experiences.

2. Recovery Is Individual

There is no single path to recovery. Treatment should be personalized to reflect each person's goals, strengths, and life experiences.

3. Emotional Healing Matters

Recovery is about more than abstinence. Addressing emotional health, relationships, self-esteem, and life purpose is essential for lasting success.

4. Growth Happens One Step at a Time

Meaningful change occurs through small, consistent choices supported by encouragement, accountability, and hope.

Why Regina Chose Behavioral Health

A Passion for Healing & Hope

Regina chose the behavioral health field because she believes every person deserves the opportunity to heal and create a life filled with hope and purpose.

She is passionate about helping individuals navigate the challenges of addiction and mental health while empowering them to recognize their strengths and build confidence in their ability to recover.

She considers it a privilege to walk alongside clients during some of the most important moments of their recovery journey and finds fulfillment in watching individuals regain confidence, reconnect with loved ones, and rediscover hope for the future.

Therapeutic Approach

Understanding Patterns, Building Skills

Regina believes counseling should help clients understand not only what they want to change, but why those patterns developed in the first place.

She works collaboratively with clients to:

  • Identify unhealthy thought patterns
  • Improve emotional awareness
  • Develop healthy coping skills
  • Strengthen relationships
  • Build confidence
  • Increase resilience
  • Prepare for long-term recovery

Her goal is to help every client leave treatment with practical tools they can continue using throughout their lives while building a healthier, more meaningful future.
