Meet Regina

Building Trust, Fostering Healing

Regina Largent is a Licensed Substance Abuse Associate (LSAA) who is passionate about helping individuals overcome addiction, strengthen their mental health, and build fulfilling lives in recovery.

She believes that healing begins with trust. By creating a safe, supportive, and respectful therapeutic environment, Regina helps clients feel comfortable exploring the challenges that have contributed to substance use while discovering the strengths they already possess.

At Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Regina provides individual counseling, facilitates group therapy, and works collaboratively with the treatment team to help clients develop the skills needed for long-term recovery.

Her compassionate approach encourages clients to recognize that recovery is not about perfection—it's about consistent progress, personal growth, and learning healthier ways to respond to life's challenges.

Professional Background

Experience in Behavioral Health & Recovery

Regina has dedicated her career to working with individuals experiencing substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship difficulties, and other behavioral health challenges.

Her experience includes helping clients identify unhealthy thinking patterns, improve emotional regulation, strengthen communication skills, and develop practical coping strategies that support lasting recovery.

She understands that many individuals entering treatment have experienced significant pain, loss, or trauma, and she believes healing occurs through patience, understanding, accountability, and evidence-based therapeutic interventions.

As part of the multidisciplinary team at Desert Mountain Healing IOP, Regina works closely with medical providers, case managers, peer support specialists, and fellow therapists to ensure clients receive coordinated, comprehensive care.

Therapeutic Approach

Compassionate, Evidence-Based Counseling

Regina's therapeutic approach combines empathy, clinical skill, and evidence-based interventions to support clients in their recovery journey.

Treatment Modalities

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Helping clients identify and change unhelpful thinking patterns and behaviors
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI): Supporting clients in exploring and resolving ambivalence about change
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Recognizing the impact of trauma and creating safety in the therapeutic relationship
  • Relapse Prevention: Building skills and strategies for maintaining long-term recovery
  • Solution-Focused Therapy: Emphasizing client strengths and solutions rather than dwelling on problems

Regina believes that effective therapy requires meeting clients where they are, honoring their experiences, and helping them recognize their own capacity for growth and change.

Areas of Focus

Specialized Support for Recovery Challenges

Regina works with clients experiencing a wide range of behavioral health challenges, including:

  • Substance use disorders (alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis)
  • Co-occurring mental health conditions (anxiety, depression, PTSD)
  • Trauma and adverse life experiences
  • Relationship and family conflict
  • Emotional regulation difficulties
  • Stress management and coping skills
  • Relapse prevention and recovery maintenance

Her holistic approach addresses not just the addiction itself, but the underlying emotional, psychological, and social factors that contribute to substance use.

Group Therapy Facilitation

Building Connection and Community

In addition to individual counseling, Regina facilitates group therapy sessions—a core component of the IOP treatment model.

Group therapy provides clients with the opportunity to connect with others who share similar experiences, learn from one another's challenges and successes, and practice new skills in a supportive, structured environment.

Benefits of Group Therapy

  • Reduces isolation and helps clients realize they're not alone
  • Provides peer support and accountability
  • Offers diverse perspectives and insights
  • Builds communication and interpersonal skills
  • Reinforces recovery concepts through shared learning

Regina creates a safe, respectful group environment where clients feel comfortable sharing their experiences and supporting one another's recovery journeys.
