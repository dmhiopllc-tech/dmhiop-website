Built By Us. Just For You.

We didn't buy a generic recovery app and slap our logo on it. We built Own Today from the ground up—specifically for Desert Mountain Healing clients.

Why? Because we're in recovery ourselves. Sean Roberts (founder, 37 years sober) and Karla Wood (founder, 9 years sober) know firsthand what actually helps in recovery—and what doesn't. Every feature in Own Today was designed by people who understand your journey because they've walked it themselves.

This isn't a third-party app we license. This is our app, created with our clinical expertise, built on our recovery experience, and updated constantly based on your feedback. When you tell us what would help, we build it.

Other IOPs might hand you a pamphlet and wish you luck. We give you a lifelong digital companion packed with tools that actually work—because we use them ourselves, and we know they make a difference.

This is the DMHIOP difference: personalized care that extends far beyond your treatment dates. You're not just getting therapy sessions—you're getting a recovery toolkit built by people who truly understand what you're going through.
