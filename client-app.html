Built By People in Recovery,
For People in Recovery

Own Today wasn't outsourced to a tech company. It was designed, built, and refined by the Desert Mountain Healing team—people who understand recovery because they live it every day.

Our founders, Sean Roberts (in recovery since 1987) and Gary Gamboa (in recovery since 2015), personally oversaw every feature, ensuring the app addresses real recovery needs, not corporate profits.

This is recovery technology built with intention, compassion, and real-world experience.
