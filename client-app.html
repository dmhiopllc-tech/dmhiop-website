Why This Matters

Recovery Doesn't Stop at 5 PM

The most dangerous moments in recovery often happen outside of treatment—late at night, on weekends, during unexpected triggers. That's when having 24/7 support in your pocket can make all the difference.

Own Today was built for those moments.

When you feel a craving at 2 AM, you can open the app and use guided meditation, chat with the AI support coach, or quickly find an emergency meeting nearby. When you're having a great day, you can track your progress and earn rewards for healthy choices. When you need accountability, the geofence check-in system helps you stay honest about meeting attendance.

This isn't just an app—it's a 24/7 recovery companion built by people in recovery, for people in recovery.

Yours to Keep, Forever

When you complete treatment at DMHIOP, you keep full access to Own Today—for life. No subscriptions, no expiration dates. We're committed to supporting your recovery journey long after discharge.
