Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Our IOP delivers structured, evidence-based treatment while allowing you to maintain work, school, and family responsibilities. You participate in 9-12 hours of group therapy and individual counseling per week, all while living at home.

  • Flexible scheduling: evening and daytime options
  • Evidence-based group therapy and individual counseling
  • Relapse prevention and coping skills training
  • Ongoing support from licensed clinicians

Evidence-based, FDA-approved

Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)

MAT combines FDA-approved medications—such as buprenorphine (Suboxone), naltrexone (Vivitrol), or methadone—with counseling and therapy to treat opioid or alcohol use disorders. Our medical team closely monitors your progress and adjusts your treatment plan as needed.

  • Reduces cravings and withdrawal symptoms
  • Increases treatment retention and long-term recovery
  • Medical oversight by licensed providers
  • Integrated with counseling and case management

Outpatient Medical Detox Assistance

If you are experiencing mild to moderate withdrawal symptoms, our outpatient medical detox assistance program provides professional oversight, symptom management, and regular check-ins—all while you remain at home.

  • Physician-supervised withdrawal management
  • Regular monitoring and symptom relief
  • Medication support when clinically appropriate
  • Seamless transition to IOP or MAT services

Counseling & Case Management

Recovery involves more than just abstinence. Our clinical team addresses co-occurring mental health concerns, life skills, and external barriers to ensure you have the support you need to succeed.

💬

Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions with a licensed therapist provide a safe space to explore personal challenges, build coping skills, and address co-occurring mental health concerns.

👥

Group Therapy

Facilitated group sessions offer peer support, shared learning, and accountability. You gain insights from others facing similar challenges and develop a community of support.

📋

Case Management

Our case managers help coordinate medical appointments, connect you with community resources, and address practical barriers such as housing, transportation, or employment.

🤝

Peer Support

Connect with trained peer recovery support specialists who have lived experience and can offer hope, encouragement, and practical guidance throughout your journey.
