Our story

Founded by People Who Understand Recovery

Desert Mountain Healing IOP was founded by Sean Roberts and Gary Gamboa because they believed addiction treatment could be delivered with greater compassion, integrity, accountability, and hope.

After experiencing recovery firsthand, they recognized that lasting recovery requires more than simply treating addiction. It requires hope, accountability, evidence-based care, and a treatment team that truly sees the person behind the diagnosis.

That belief became the foundation of Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Today, every client who walks through our doors is treated as an individual—not a number. We believe recovery is possible for everyone, and our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence-based treatment that empowers people to rebuild their lives.

Recovery begins with hope

Whether someone is seeking help for the first time or returning after a relapse, they will be welcomed without judgment and supported every step of the way.

Recovery begins with hope, grows through connection, and succeeds through compassionate care.

Core treatment services

Care for Every Stage of Recovery

Our team helps each client identify the combination of services that best supports their needs and recovery goals.

01

Intensive Outpatient Program

Structured treatment that includes group counseling, individual support, relapse-prevention education, life-skills development, and recovery planning while clients continue living at home.

DMHIOP offers morning and evening IOP programming Monday through Friday to help clients balance recovery with work and family responsibilities.

  • Group counseling and recovery education
  • Individual clinical support
  • Relapse prevention planning
  • Life skills and coping strategies
  • Morning and evening schedules
Explore IOP benefits
02

Medication-Assisted Treatment

When clinically appropriate, medication may be combined with counseling, IOP, case management, and ongoing recovery support for opioid or alcohol use disorders.

Our approach connects medical care with behavioral health treatment so clients receive support for both the physical and emotional parts of recovery.

  • Nurse Practitioner oversight
  • Medication management & monitoring
  • Integrated with IOP and counseling
  • Individualized treatment plans
  • Long-term recovery support
Learn about MAT
03

Outpatient Medical Detox Assistance

Nurse Practitioner assessment and outpatient medical support may help clinically appropriate clients manage withdrawal symptoms while beginning counseling and recovery services.

Suitability for outpatient detox assistance is based on an individualized medical and clinical assessment.

  • Medical assessment and monitoring
  • Symptom management support
  • Concurrent counseling services
  • Safe, supervised care
  • Transition planning to ongoing treatment
Learn about detox assistance
Additional recovery support

More Than a Single Service

Recovery often becomes stronger when clinical care is supported by practical help, healthy connection, and continued skill-building.

1:1

Individual Counseling

Address triggers, coping skills, emotional health, relationships, personal goals, and barriers to sustained recovery.

GRP

Group Therapy & Education

Develop recovery skills, communication, accountability, insight, and peer connection in a supportive setting.

CM

Case Management

Receive help addressing employment, benefits, transportation, healthcare, legal obligations, housing, and other practical needs.

RP

Relapse Prevention

Recognize warning signs, identify triggers, strengthen coping strategies, and create practical plans for high-risk situations.

FAM

Family & Recovery Support

When appropriate, families can receive education and support to improve communication and establish healthier boundaries.

PEER

Peer & Community Support

Build healthier connections and participate in recovery-focused activities that support long-term stability.
