One connected treatment plan

Support That Works Together

Desert Mountain Healing IOP provides coordinated outpatient addiction treatment for adults who need structure, medical support, counseling, and practical assistance while continuing to live at home.

Rather than treating each service as a separate program, our team builds one individualized plan around each client’s clinical needs, recovery goals, responsibilities, and level of support.

Clients may begin group treatment, counseling, case management, medication support, and recovery planning from the beginning of care.

Core treatment services

Care for Every Stage of Recovery

Our team helps each client identify the combination of services that best supports their needs and recovery goals.

01

Intensive Outpatient Program

Structured treatment that includes group counseling, individual support, relapse-prevention education, life-skills development, and recovery planning while clients continue living at home.

DMHIOP offers morning and evening IOP programming Monday through Friday to help clients balance recovery with work and family responsibilities.

Explore IOP benefits →
02

Medication-Assisted Treatment

When clinically appropriate, medication may be combined with counseling, IOP, case management, and ongoing recovery support for opioid or alcohol use disorders.

Our approach connects medical care with behavioral health treatment so clients receive support for both the physical and emotional parts of recovery.

Learn about MAT →
03

Outpatient Medical Detox Assistance

Nurse Practitioner assessment and outpatient medical support may help clinically appropriate clients manage withdrawal symptoms while beginning counseling and recovery services.

Suitability for outpatient detox assistance is based on an individualized medical and clinical assessment.

Common questions

Getting Started with Treatment

Can I continue working while attending treatment?

Many clients continue working or caring for family while participating in outpatient treatment. Morning and evening IOP options can make scheduling more manageable.

How do I know which services I need?

The recommended combination of services is based on an individualized assessment of your clinical needs, health, goals, schedule, and available support.

Do you offer medication-assisted treatment?

Yes. When clinically appropriate, medication may be combined with counseling, structured outpatient care, case management, and continued recovery support.

Can you verify my insurance before I begin?

Yes. Our team can review available benefits and explain likely next steps. Insurance verification does not guarantee payment or authorization.
