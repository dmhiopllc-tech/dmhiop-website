Comprehensive Support for Lasting Recovery

Desert Mountain Healing IOP provides coordinated outpatient addiction treatment for adults who need structure, medical support, counseling, and practical assistance while continuing to live at home. Our services work together as one individualized treatment plan rather than as disconnected programs.

Clients may begin counseling, group treatment, case management, medication support, and recovery planning from the beginning of care. The exact combination of services is based on each person’s clinical needs, recovery goals, schedule, and level of support.

Exclusive to Desert Mountain Healing IOP

Recovery Support Beyond the Therapy Room

Recovery happens every day—not only during group or counseling sessions. That is why DMHIOP developed its own client engagement app specifically for people enrolled in our program.

The DMHIOP Client App helps clients stay connected, organized, and actively engaged in recovery between appointments. It was built in-house around the needs of our clients and the way our program supports lasting change.

Our Core Treatment Services

Our team helps each client identify the combination of services that best supports their needs and recovery goals.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Structured treatment that includes group counseling, individual support, relapse-prevention education, and recovery planning while clients continue living at home.

Learn About IOP Benefits →

Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)

When clinically appropriate, medication may be combined with counseling, IOP, case management, and ongoing recovery support for opioid or alcohol use disorders.

Learn About MAT →

Outpatient Medical Detox Assistance

Nurse Practitioner assessment and outpatient medical support may help appropriate clients manage withdrawal symptoms while beginning treatment and recovery services.

Learn About Detox Assistance →

Individual Counseling

One-on-one counseling helps clients address triggers, coping skills, relationships, personal goals, emotional health, and barriers to sustained recovery.

Ask About Individual Counseling →

Group Therapy and Education

Facilitated groups provide education, accountability, peer connection, communication practice, and opportunities to apply recovery skills in a supportive setting.

Ask About Group Services →

Case Management

Case management helps clients address employment, transportation, benefits, healthcare access, legal obligations, housing concerns, and other practical recovery needs.

Ask About Case Management →

Relapse Prevention

Clients learn to recognize warning signs, identify triggers, strengthen coping strategies, build sober support, and create practical plans for high-risk situations.

Family and Recovery Support

When appropriate, treatment can include education and support that helps families understand recovery, improve communication, and establish healthier boundaries.

Insurance and Getting Started

We work with a range of insurance plans and can review your benefits before admission. Coverage depends on your specific plan, eligibility, authorization requirements, deductibles, copayments, and medical-necessity criteria.

Use our online insurance verification form or contact our office to speak with a member of our team. Verification of benefits is not a guarantee of payment by an insurance company.
