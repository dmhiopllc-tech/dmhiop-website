Recovery built this place

Created From Experience, Purpose, and Hope

Desert Mountain Healing IOP was founded by Sean Roberts and Gary Gamboa, two men whose own recovery journeys shaped a shared vision for addiction treatment in New Mexico.

They believed treatment should remain focused on the person seeking help. In 2022, after years of discussing how care could be more compassionate, practical, and recovery-centered, they invested their own resources and opened Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

They did not open a treatment center because they wanted to own one. They opened it because they wanted to make a difference.

That original commitment continues to guide the organization today: recovery comes first, people matter, and every client deserves a real opportunity to rebuild their life.
