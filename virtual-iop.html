Understanding virtual IOP

Who Can Benefit From Virtual Services?

Virtual IOP may be a good fit for clients whose individual circumstances make in-person attendance challenging.

Virtual IOP May Be Right If You:

While our in-person program remains our primary offering, we recognize that some clients may benefit from virtual participation when certain practical barriers exist.

  • Live a considerable distance from our Rio Rancho location
  • Have work or school commitments that conflict with in-person session times
  • Face health considerations that make in-person attendance difficult
  • Experience transportation challenges that cannot be resolved through available resources

Virtual IOP Schedule

Our virtual IOP follows a structured 90-day course of care, with clients scheduled up to five days per week based on clinical needs and their individualized treatment plan. Choose morning or evening sessions to accommodate your work and family commitments.
