What makes us different

Staff Who Understand Recovery Personally

Desert Mountain Healing IOP was founded by Sean Roberts and Gary Gamboa because they believed addiction treatment could be delivered with greater compassion, integrity, accountability, and hope.

After experiencing recovery firsthand, they recognized that lasting recovery requires more than simply treating addiction. It requires hope, accountability, evidence-based care, and a treatment team that truly sees the person behind the diagnosis.

That belief became the foundation of Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Many of our staff members have walked the path of recovery themselves. This combination of professional expertise and lived experience creates a unique therapeutic environment where clients are seen, heard, and understood.

Why This Matters

  • Authentic Understanding Staff with personal recovery experience understand the struggles, triggers, and triumphs of addiction recovery
  • Empathy & Compassion We meet clients with non-judgmental support and genuine care
  • Medical & Therapeutic Expertise Our team includes medical professionals, licensed therapists, counselors, and peer support specialists
  • Community of Peers Group therapy connects you with others who are also in recovery, creating lasting support networks
  • Personalized Care Plans We tailor treatment to your unique needs, challenges, and goals
  • You're Not a Number We remember your name, not your patient number — because you matter

Recovery is possible. You don't have to do it alone.

You will be welcomed here

Treatment Without Judgment

Whether you're seeking help for the first time or returning after a relapse, you will be welcomed without judgment at Desert Mountain Healing IOP.

Our team understands the courage it takes to ask for help. We know that recovery isn't linear, that setbacks happen, and that what matters most is taking the next right step forward.

We believe recovery is possible for everyone, and our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence-based treatment that empowers people to rebuild their lives.

As clients consistently share in their reviews: "They took me in with no judgment at all." "They remember your name not your patient number." "These people are amazing."

What You Can Expect

Compassionate Welcome: From your first contact, you'll be treated with dignity and respect

Individualized Assessment: We take time to understand your unique situation and needs

Collaborative Treatment Planning: You're an active participant in your recovery journey

Evidence-Based Care: Proven therapies combined with personalized support

Ongoing Support: We're with you through IOP, step-down care, and continuing recovery

Recovery begins with hope, grows through connection, and succeeds through compassionate care.
